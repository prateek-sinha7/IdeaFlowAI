Codebase Map

The repository splits into backend/ (Python), frontend/ (Next.js), infra/ (Terraform), skills/ + specs/ + docs/ (content & specs), and .planning/ (the 22-phase history). Annotated below.

In plain terms. A quick tour of where things live so you know which door to open. The backend is two Python packages — app (the web/IO layer) and agents (the runtime + config). The frontend is a Next.js app, and infra is the Terraform. The single most important rule is that agents' inner layers are forbidden from importing app — that one-way arrow is the whole architecture in one line.
What it is

An annotated map of the repository's folders and their responsibilities.

Why it exists

To orient you before diving into a file, and to make the layer boundaries visible.

How it works

backend/ = app + agents; frontend/ = Next.js; infra/ = Terraform; import-linter enforces the inner-layer boundaries.
