# FIX-728

## What was wrong

This fix is for bug report QA-KB-068. The root cause is from the 2026-10-02 analysis on
[ISS-868](20261001-2106-ISS-868.md). It was not re-derived here. Every anchor was re-read on the working tree
(HEAD `64d7b8429`) before editing.

The review gates have two registries that share the `review:` key space:

- `set_review_response` writes the response to `_questionnaire_responses["review:{gate_key}"]` (`store.py:162-163`).
- The matching event goes in `_resume_events` under the same key (`:133-135`).

[FIX-152](20260730-1825-FIX-152.md) D4's `forget_run` popped the bare-`run_id` clarify slot, the force-proceed flag and
the bare event. Its review scan, though, iterated `self._resume_events` only (`store.py:218-225` before this fix). As a
result, every gate response outlived its run until process exit: one dict per `(run, agent, visit)` slot, holding the
user's verbatim `edited_content` and `instructions`.

Blast radius, re-checked by grep before editing:

- `ArtifactStore.forget_run` has one caller, `run_engine.py:136` (`_cleanup_pipeline`). `run_commands.py:745` calls the
  StateMachine's `forget_run`, not this one.
- The review slot has one reader, `get_review_response` at `engine.py:12505`. The card quotes it at `:12481`; the
  anchor has shifted by 24 lines.

## What changed, and why there

**`backend/agents/artifact_store/store.py`, `ArtifactStore.forget_run` (`:198-225`).** The single-registry
`stale_keys` comprehension is replaced by one predicate over both registries:

    review_key = f"review:{run_id}"
    review_prefix = f"{review_key}:"
    for registry in (self._resume_events, self._questionnaire_responses):
        for k in [k for k in registry if k == review_key or k.startswith(review_prefix)]:
            registry.pop(k, None)

The docstring no longer limits the review eviction to asyncio.Event entries, which was ISS-868's constraint 4. It also
states the run-end-only precondition (constraint 1). The fix lives in the one function every run-end path reaches, as
the card placed it. It adds no import, makes no app-layer reach into the private dict (constraint 2), and changes
nothing under `agents/execution_engine/`. No other production line changed.

## Deliberately not done

- **[ISS-1003](20261002-0516-ISS-1003.md) stays open and untouched.** It covers the bare `pass` at
  `run_engine.py:137-138` / `:143-144` and the missing wiring test. Three reasons:
  - It has no red test (`verification.test_files: []`).
  - Its own card calls the fix a judgement call ("If the owner decides a silent best-effort cleanup is intended,
    close this as won't-fix").
  - Its concrete hazard for this change, a loop that mutates the dict it scans, is avoided here by iterating a list
    copy.
- **The test file was not edited.** The 4-test-writer's test asserts only the review-response cell. The analyzer also
  asked for assertions that the review events, the bare clarify slot and the force-proceed flag are gone. Those are
  covered by the in-process probe below, not by a committed test. The `xfail(strict=True)` marker is left for the
  6-verifier.
