## What changed
- `backend/agents/workflows/compiler.py` and `backend/agents/execution_engine/budget.py`: comment and docstring text only,
  made by the backend step (`.agents/tasks/workflow-gaps/backend-record.md`). The contradictory trust block is now one
  posture. "user/db" shorthand in `compile`, `_compile_limits` and the limits comments now reads user, or file/builtin/db.
  The `_SAVED_TRUST` branches are unchanged and stay inert while `db` is in `_TRUSTED_SOURCES`.
- `.knowledge/architecture/DOMAIN-workflow-compilation.md`, prose above the generated block only (see the record file for
  the exact lines): the `CompiledWorkflow` field list names `capabilities`; the failure notes about `user_allowed` and
  `spawn_subagents` name untrusted `user` manifests instead of "user/db"; the "Trust is a parameter" paragraph states the
  current posture; the limits paragraph states the 8, 2, 900 and 5 defaults.
