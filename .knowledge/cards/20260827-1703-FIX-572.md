## Why this could not be fixed in the AGENT.md

`prototype-validate` and `prototype-revision-analyzer` both declared
`model: us.anthropic.claude-sonnet-5`. That is correct on local dev
(`us-east-2`) and a hard failure on production (`eu-central-1`) — the `us.`
inference-profile prefix simply does not exist there. Editing the manifests
just moves the breakage to the other environment: there is no single literal
that is right in both.

So the region has to be applied at resolve time, from `settings.AWS_REGION`,
and the manifest has to stay region-free.

## Why the end of resolve(), and not earlier

`ModelResolver.resolve()` runs a five-tier precedence chain and then validates
the winner against the catalog. Normalizing before that would mean each tier
had to be normalized separately and the catalog would need every geo variant of
every id to stay valid. Normalizing after means one call covers every tier, and
the catalog only needs the variants that a human might type as a manual
override — which is why `eu.anthropic.claude-sonnet-5` was added to
`model_catalog.py` rather than the whole cross product.

## The failure mode it removes

A wrong geo prefix surfaces as a Bedrock `ValidationException`, which
`_is_transient_throttle` deliberately does NOT classify as retryable. So the
fallback chain never engages and the step fails before emitting a token — a
run that looks like it produced nothing rather than one that reports a bad
model id.
