## Context

The conditional gate could already route on a human's decision, but nothing
told the UI what the legal decisions were. The human saw a free-text review box
and had to type a value that happened to match a declared outcome.

Worse, when `condition_agent` was left to default, the gate parsed the GATED
step's model output — so a model had to re-emit a decision the human had
already made. Observed live on `ex_A4_human_gate`: the model answered "Hola"
instead of `{"decision":"spanish"}` and the run failed closed to GATE_BLOCK.

## Decision

**1. A routed gate publishes its outcomes.** In
`kernel_services.run_human_gate`, when the step declares a route and the gate
has no payload of its own, the review payload becomes
`{"prompt": <step output>, "choices": [<outcomes>]}` and `artifact_kind` is set
to `"conditional_gate"`. Both are additive — a gate on a step with no route is
untouched, so every existing HITL gate keeps its current payload and its
current empty `artifact_kind`.

**2. The click is the decision.** `InlineGateActions` renders
`artifact_kind="conditional_gate"` as one chip per declared outcome and submits
`{"decision": "<outcome>"}` as the gate edit. Approve/Edit are hidden for that
kind; "Request changes" is kept.

**3. `condition_agent` names the human's own artifact.** On `ex_A4_human_gate`
the route reads `condition_agent: ask`, and `ask` declares
`produces: ["route_decision"]` with `pick-language` consuming the same kind.
R-27 requires the route's resolved decision source to declare the typed kind
the gate reads, and `ask` IS the real source: the human's chip click is written
into ITS artifact by `_apply_declared_gate_edit`, which rewrites the PREVIOUS
step's artifact.

## Why this shape

Pointing `condition_agent` at the pre-gate step takes the model out of the
decision path entirely. Verified: routing stays correct with non-JSON prose, an
apology, and a zero-character model reply.

The `produces`/`consumes` pair had to move together — a pair that disagrees is
unsatisfiable and the run dies at `workflow_validated`.

## Consequences

- A human-decided branch needs a placeholder step whose only job is to BE the
  artifact the human overwrites. `ex_A4_human_gate`'s `ask` step exists for
  exactly this and answers `"(awaiting language choice)"`.
- Approving WITHOUT editing leaves the placeholder in place; the route then
  finds no matching outcome and, with no `default_next`, the run ends at the
  gate. That is by design.
- Consumers working in composed-id space must normalise route targets
  (ADR-0017) — unchanged by this decision.

## Verification

Verified end to end on A4: the human picks Spanish at the gate, the run routes
to `say-hola` and skips the English and Dutch branches. Tests: test_gates 46,
test_declared_gate_streaming 3, test_conditional_branch_route_t25 3 — green.
