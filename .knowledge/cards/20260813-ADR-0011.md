# ADR-0011

## Decision

In the context of custom workflows whose steps hand files to one another, facing step
prompts that name their own filenames ("save it to paragraph.md") while the engine
writes and reads a machine-derived artifact name, we decided the delivery block's
`artifact_name(instance_id, topic)` is AUTHORITATIVE and a filename written into a step
prompt is not honoured, to achieve one filename that the artifact guarantee, the roster
and the deliverable readback all agree on, accepting that a user-authored filename is
silently ignored and that inter-step handoff must therefore travel through the roster
rather than through names the author chose.

**Area:** engine, factory, workflow
