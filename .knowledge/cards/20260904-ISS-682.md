## Related

## The gap

FIX-535 made step objects survive a Composer load/save round trip.
`buildWorkflowManifest` gained a `sourceManifest` parameter to do the same for
the manifest's own top-level keys, but no caller supplies it. `id`,
`display_name`, `limits`, `input_providers` and `seed_files` are therefore still
dropped on save.

Recorded by the fixing commit itself (`00b61ff93`) as a known, deliberate gap.
