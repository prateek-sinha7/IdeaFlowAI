## Related

## What was wrong

Wave workers were not rendered as nested children and their individual states 
(progress, skip, repair) were not visible in the UI. Users had no way to see 
individual worker progress within a wave or take per-worker actions.

Wave workers (tasks dispatched in parallel within a wave) need UI visibility 
as nested children under the parent wave, with individual progress, skip and 
repair state tracking. Currently they render at the same level as the parent 
wave or are grouped without clear hierarchy.

## The fix (commit 1e329e47b)

Landed in commit `1e329e47b` with the files listed under `globs`; the tests under `verification.test_files` pin the behaviour.
