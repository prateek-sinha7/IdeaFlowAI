# ADR-0003

## Decision

In the context of a run's agent roster being built twice — once from the compiled
manifest and once from registry membership — facing a run-entry assertion that a
composed workflow can never satisfy because its steps are template instances with no
AGENT.md, we decided the compiled plan is the single source of the roster and the
assertion is deleted rather than relaxed, to achieve one roster that composed workflows
can populate, accepting the loss of a cross-file drift guard that nothing yet replaces.

**Area:** engine, registry, workflow
