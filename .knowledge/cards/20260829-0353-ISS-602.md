## Summary

Deferred finding from [FIX-398](20260829-0352-FIX-398.md), recorded so it is not rediscovered.

FIX-398 introduced `frontend/src/components/ui/PasswordInput.tsx` and routed the six password
fields named by [ISS-338](20260828-2050-ISS-338.md), [ISS-576](20260829-0218-ISS-576.md) and
[ISS-577](20260829-0219-ISS-577.md) through it. After that change,
`grep -rn 'type="password"' frontend/src` (excluding tests) returns exactly one remaining
hand-rolled occurrence: the main sign-in form's Password field at
`frontend/src/app/login/page.tsx:303-317`.

## Why it was left

- No ISS card names it. FIX-398's brief was the six fields the three cards enumerate, and
  converting a seventh would have been a change no card asked for.
- Its markup is a different shape from the other six: the input sits inside an
  `input-focus relative` wrapper that already holds an absolutely-positioned `Lock` icon at
  `left-3.5` and uses `pl-10 pr-3.5` padding. Dropping in `PasswordInput` as-is would nest a
  second `relative` wrapper and double up the padding, so it needs a deliberate decision about
  whether the primitive should accept a leading adornment rather than a mechanical swap.

## Disposition

Open, low priority. Either extend `PasswordInput` to take an optional leading icon and convert
this field, or decide that a single-entry sign-in field does not need a reveal affordance and say
so — but decide it rather than leaving it as the one field nobody looked at.
