# ISS-066

## Title

Register drift: ISS-054..ISS-061 have NO rows in ISSUES-REGISTER.md, yet those ids are live in FIX-190/192/193/194/195 rows, in cards, and in commit messages. ISS-055 is a free gap inside a used range. Consequence: a register-only "next id" lookup returns a number that is already taken — which is exactly how ISS-050 was re-issued for an unrelated defect on 2026-08-11 and had to be renumbered to ISS-064. Backfill the missing rows (or record why they are absent) so the register is once again a usable allocator.
