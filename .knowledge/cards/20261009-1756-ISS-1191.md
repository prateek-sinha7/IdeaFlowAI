## The failure

`tests/agents/test_engine_cancel.py::test_genuine_cancel_still_raises_and_releases`, `--tb=short`:

```
    cancelled_ev = await gen.athrow(asyncio.CancelledError())
E   asyncio.exceptions.CancelledError
```

The test's docstring (`:200-205`) and its comments (`:219-223`) expect the following. With no `cancel_event` (the genuine,
non-cooperative branch), the `athrow` returns one `pipeline_cancelled` event. The next `__anext__` then re-raises
`CancelledError`, and the session release must have fired once (`assert calls == [run_id]`, `:231`). What actually happens is
that the `CancelledError` leaves the generator on the `athrow` itself (`:224`), before any event is yielded.
