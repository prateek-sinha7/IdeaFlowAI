## What was wrong

Bug report QA-KB-021. Two defects, both taken from the analysis cards and not re-derived here.

- [ISS-682](20260904-ISS-682.md), the root card. The two `setInitialSavedComposition({...})` literals that seed a
  saved row on a cold mount, `frontend/src/app/[...view]/page.tsx:3776` (`/workflows/{id}/edit`) and `:3978`
  (`/workflows/{id}/run`) (pre-change lines), set `manifestSteps: saved.manifest?.steps` but no `manifest`. So
  `DashboardLayout.tsx:3237` `initialManifest={savedComposition?.manifest}` passed `undefined`, and
  `manifestTopLevelExtra(undefined)` returned `{}` (`frontend/src/store/api/userWorkflows.ts:617-618`). On Save the
  PATCH body's manifest then held only `steps` and the modeled keys, and the server replaces `manifest_json`
  outright. `limits`, `input_providers`, `seed_files`, `id`, `display_name` and every other unmodeled top-level key
  were dropped. The click path (`DashboardLayout.tsx:1720`, `handleLaunchSaved`) already carried the line, but the
  analysis showed a real open ends on the page.tsx seeds.
- [ISS-875](20261001-2110-ISS-875.md), the sibling. `ComposerPage.tsx:442` was
  `useState<WorkflowCapabilities>({})`, and the only writer was the Internet toggle. `capabilities` is in
  `MODELED_MANIFEST_KEYS` (`userWorkflows.ts:606-614`), so the passthrough skips it on purpose, and
  `buildWorkflowManifest` writes it only when the state is non-empty (`userWorkflows.ts:647-649`). A workflow saved
  with Internet on reopened with the toggle off, and its next Save wrote no `capabilities`.

## What changed, and why there

1. `frontend/src/app/[...view]/page.tsx`: added
   `manifest: (saved.manifest as Record<string, unknown> | undefined) ?? null,` beside
   `manifestSteps: saved.manifest?.steps` in the `/edit` literal and the `/run` literal. They were at `:3784` and
   `:3985` when this was written; a concurrent edit above them (ISS-870, `:1340`) had already shifted every line by
   3. This is the line `DashboardLayout.tsx:1720` already carries. A grep of `setInitialSavedComposition(` and
   `setSavedComposition(` outside tests finds four seeds of `SavedComposition`: `page.tsx:521`, `:3779`, `:3982`
   and `DashboardLayout.tsx:1717`. The other hits are resets to `null` (`page.tsx:555`, `DashboardLayout.tsx:1570`)
   and the sync (`DashboardLayout.tsx:1555`). `savedComposition.manifest` has one
   reader, `DashboardLayout.tsx:3237`. Every row-backed seed now carries the manifest. `buildWorkflowManifest` already
   does the right thing when it gets a source, so the defect was in what fed it, not in the function. The card
   considered a shared seed builder and rejected it (ISS-682, Fix, refinement 4), so the two-line diff stands.
2. The built-in canvas seed at `page.tsx:521` is untouched. It carries no `id` and no `manifest`, as the
   [ADR-0014](20260824-1630-ADR-0014.md) locked constraint requires.
3. `frontend/src/components/workflow/composer/ComposerPage.tsx:444-446`: the state is now
   `useState<WorkflowCapabilities>((initialManifest?.capabilities as WorkflowCapabilities | undefined) ?? {})`, with
   a two-line comment at `:442-443`. A `useState` initializer is enough, because `DashboardLayout.tsx:3212`
   (`key={savedComposition?.id ?? "new"}`) remounts `ComposerPage` when the row arrives. `capabilities` was not
   added to the passthrough, as the card says. The toggle still writes through `onCapabilitiesChange={setCapabilities}`.
   A built-in canvas or a new composition has no `initialManifest`, so it starts at `{}` as before.

Not taken, by design:

- The card's optional hygiene: `initialManifest` is still missing from `handleSave`'s dependency list. ISS-682 rates
  it harmless because of the key remount.
- `DashboardLayout.tsx:1720` is redundant in practice but stays. Removing it is out of scope.

## Not fixed: ISS-876 (left open)

[ISS-876](20261001-2110-ISS-876.md): "Save as copy" on a built-in canvas still loses the built-in's `limits` and
`input_providers`. There is no source for them. `GET /api/workflows/{id}` (`WorkflowDetail`,
`backend/app/api/workflows.py:226-254`) exposes none of the top-level keys, and the canvas seed may not carry an `id`.
The fix needs a backend payload change, plus a product answer to whether a copy inherits `limits` and
`input_providers`. It is not part of this fix, and the card stays `open`.
