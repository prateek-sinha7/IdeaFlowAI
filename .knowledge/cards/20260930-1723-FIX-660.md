## Bug

A run failed, was resumed, and completed. The header still named the step from the earlier `pipeline_failed`, because `failedAgents` survived both the resume and the completion.

## Fix

`useWorkflow.ts` clears `failedAgents` on pipeline completion, and `failedAgents` plus `degradedFailedAgents` on resume.
