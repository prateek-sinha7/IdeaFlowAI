## Related

## What was wrong

`saveUserWorkflow` strips `agent_ids` on update — correct for the legacy
agent_ids-backed rows, whose composition is immutable after create, but a
Composer row is edited through `manifest_json` alone. Its `agent_ids` column
therefore stayed at whatever the row was created with, and the Saved Workflows
card (and the page total, which summed the same field) showed "4 agents" for a
12-step workflow.

## The fix (commit 97717eb9f)

`user_workflows.py` derives `step_count` from `len(manifest_json["steps"])`
when the row is manifest-shaped and falls back to `agent_ids` otherwise.
