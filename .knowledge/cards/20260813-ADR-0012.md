# ADR-0012

## Decision

In the context of a Composer offering per-node tool grants, facing four toggles that the
runtime cannot honour — two inert, one whose "off" position only disabled secret
scanning, and two that made the workflow fail validation on save — we decided the grants
are FIXED (`read_files` and `write_files` always on, `exec` and `spawn_subagents` always
off) and no longer author- editable, to achieve a manifest that states what the runtime
actually does, accepting that per-step privilege scoping is now a design question rather
than a half-working feature.

**Area:** frontend, factory, workflow
