# ISS-987

Found while regression-checking the [ISS-949](20261002-0107-ISS-949.md) fix (QA-KB-061) on the test files that touch the
source-id paths. Not a sibling by mechanism, so no INFERRED label applies: this is an adjacent defect, observed. It matters
to that analysis because these are the only tests that pin what `_mint_revision_row` writes into `source_run_id`, the
question [ISS-986](20261002-0432-ISS-986.md) leaves open, and all three are dead.

## What happens (CONFIRMED, observed 2026-10-02, shipped working tree)

- `backend/tests/agents/test_phase5_chaining.py`: 2 failed, 17 passed. The two are `TestSourceRunIdWritten::test_mint_revision_row_writes_source_run_id`
  (call at `:249`) and `::test_source_run_id_written_on_revision_row` (calls at `:325`, `:339`).
- `backend/tests/agents/test_chain_context_v3.py`: 1 failed, 14 passed. It is `TestFR020SourceRunIdWritten::test_revision_mint_writes_source_run_id_equals_parent`
  (call at `:139`).
- All three raise `TypeError: _mint_revision_row() missing 1 required keyword-only argument: 'caller_tier'`.

## Why

`b2a621f84` (2026-09-17, "Fix/cognito login (#8)") made `caller_tier: str` a required keyword-only parameter of
`_mint_revision_row` (`backend/app/api/run_commands.py:4604-4614`). Its docstring (`:4635-4642`) says why: the caller's EFFECTIVE tier
comes from the verified token, "Required, not derived from `user.tier`" (Decision 3 of the Cognito-only auth migration). The three tests
pre-date it, call `_mint_revision_row(db, user=..., parent_run_id=..., ...)`, and `grep caller_tier` over both files is empty.
They give their user a tier (`tier="basic"` at `test_phase5_chaining.py:247`, `user.tier = "pro"` at `test_chain_context_v3.py:135`),
which the mint no longer reads. The product is right and the tests are stale.

Observed with an in-process probe that edits no repo file (`evidence/qa-kb-061/01-analyze-probe-output.txt` section 6, `PROBE_FIX_D`:
`_mint_revision_row` gets `caller_tier="pro"` when a caller omits it): `test_phase5_chaining.py` 19 passed and `test_chain_context_v3.py`
15 passed. So supplying `caller_tier` is the whole repair, and the assertions behind it hold today: the minted row has both
`source_run_id` and `parent_run_id` equal to the parent.

[FIX-697](20261001-2237-FIX-697.md) already listed `test_phase5_chaining.py::TestSourceRunIdWritten (2)` among its pre-existing failures and
recorded them "here only"; no ISS card or ledger row carries any of the three.

## Fix direction

The tests bend to the app: pass `caller_tier` in the three calls, matching the tier each test already gives its user (the probe used
`"pro"` for all three; `"basic"` for the `test_phase5_chaining.py` pair was not run). Do not give `caller_tier` a default in
`_mint_revision_row`, which would reopen the hole Decision 3 closed. Once green, these tests are the guard for the `source_run_id` write that
[ISS-986](20261002-0432-ISS-986.md) asks the fixer to reason about.
