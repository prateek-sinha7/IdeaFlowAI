## What was wrong
Gap 4 of the workflow-gaps review. `plan.py` defined `RepoSpec(url, ref, branch, index)` ("INERT in Phase 4") and
`CompiledWorkflow.repo: RepoSpec | None = None`. `repo` is not in `_ALLOWED_TOP_KEYS`, `WorkflowManifest` has no `repo`
field, and a manifest with a top-level `repo` fails with `ManifestValidationError: unknown top-level key(s) ['repo']`.
The compiler never passed `repo=`. A search of `agents/`, `app/` and `tests/` found no reader of `compiled.repo` or
`RepoSpec(...)`. Comments in `engine.py` (about `:5257`) and `integration_providers/providers.py` (about `:68`) mentioned a
"RepoSpec injection (09-04)" that does not exist in code. Brownfield workflows (`sample_brownfield`) work without the
field: the `repo` context provider reads `ctx.repo_source_run_id` and `ctx.context_pack_target`, and `repo_diff` reads
the runner workspace.
## Decision
The user approved Option A on 2026-11-03 (`.agents/tasks/workflow-gaps/decisions.md`): delete the dead field. Exposing
`repo` to authors was rejected because an authorable clone URL needs SSRF and credential controls and a consumer.
## What changed
- `plan.py`: deleted `RepoSpec` and `CompiledWorkflow.repo`; header and docstring mentions updated.
- Comments fixed in `engine.py`, `providers.py` and `backend/app/agents/runtime/local.py` (about `:416`, the same stale
  reference, not named in the decision).
- `docs/workflow-manifest.schema.json`: the "Not authorable: repo" sentence no longer mentions `RepoSpec`.
- No schema migration. No test constructed `CompiledWorkflow(repo=...)`.
All 39 manifests compile to the same plan as before apart from `capabilities` ([FIX-787](20261103-1705-FIX-787.md)); the
only difference in `dataclasses.asdict` output is the removed `repo: None` key, and the hashes match for 39 of 39 once
that key is re-inserted.
