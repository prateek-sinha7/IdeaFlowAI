# FIX-330

**Register source:** `BUG-20260828-015930-runs-cancelled` in `bug-hunter/ledger.md`.

## What was wrong

Clicking "Run Again" on a cancelled run navigated to `/runs/{id}/stream` and the
live view stayed frozen on "Running · 0/4 agents · Starting…" for 15-30+ seconds
after the backend had already emitted `pipeline_failed` — reproduced 2 of 3
cold-start cycles by the validator, always cured by a manual reload
([ISS-220](20260828-1712-ISS-220.md)). The same entry point is shared by the failed
run's "Reopen & fix from the failed step" ([ISS-236](20260828-1740-ISS-236.md)) and
the degraded run's "Run again" ([ISS-237](20260828-1741-ISS-237.md)) — all three
buttons are wired to the one `onRelaunch={handleResumeRun}` prop
(`frontend/src/components/layout/DashboardLayout.tsx:3012`).

## Root cause

`frontend/src/providers/RunConnectionProvider.tsx:331-339` (pre-change) —
`fanout` was a plain `subscribersRef.current.forEach((fn) => fn(msg))` with no
buffering and no last-frame cache.

The chain, as confirmed on ISS-220's analyzer pass and re-checked here:

1. `handleResumeRun` (`DashboardLayout.tsx:2253`) resumes and calls
   `runConnection.attachRun(run_id)` (line 2273).
2. The `pipelineState?.isRunning` effect (`DashboardLayout.tsx:735-769`) pushes
   `router.push(routes.runStream(incomingRunId))` (line 768) with no
   `onSwitchToLiveRun` first, remounting `frontend/src/app/[...view]/page.tsx`.
3. That remount tears down page.tsx's two fan-out listeners — the pipeline reducer
   (`page.tsx:2210-2220`) and the chat transcript (`chatSubscribe`,
   `page.tsx:2231`) — and rebuilds them on mount. Between the unmount cleanup and
   the mount effect, `subscribersRef` is EMPTY.
4. Any frame `fanout` dispatched in that window went to nobody and was gone for
   good; the one compensating mechanism, the T11 `liveRunIds` fast path's durable
   replay (`page.tsx:3299-3419`), is a single non-retried `getRunEvents` snapshot.

The transport itself is not at fault: `RunStreamConnection`/`useRunStream` live in
the provider subtree above the router, so frames keep arriving throughout the
remount — only the page-local subscriber wiring drops out.

## What changed and why there

One file, `frontend/src/providers/RunConnectionProvider.tsx` — the shared seam every
resume path funnels through, per ISS-220's "where the fix belongs" (option a). No
change in `handleResumeRun`, `DashboardLayout` or `RunChatLane`: a per-button or
per-caller guard would leave the two sibling entry points and any future caller of
the same `isRunning` effect exposed to the identical race.

- `fanout` (now `:349-372`) — when `subscribersRef.current.size === 0` the frame is
  pushed onto `gapFramesRef` instead of being dropped, ageing out entries older than
  `GAP_REPLAY_MS` and capping the buffer at `GAP_REPLAY_MAX`. A fan-out with
  subscribers present clears the buffer first: delivery is live again, so the held
  frames are replayed exactly once.
- `subscribe` (now `:377-398`) — a joining listener is handed the still-fresh held
  frames synchronously before it returns its unsubscribe. Both listeners page.tsx
  registers in the same mount commit receive them (React flushes a commit's effects
  without interleaving a network callback), and the downstream reducers already dedup
  by `event_id` and run-scope by `_sourceRunId`.
- `GAP_REPLAY_MAX = 200` / `GAP_REPLAY_MS = 15_000` (`:74-90`) — bounds. A remount
  gap is sub-second; the window only has to cover a route commit. The bounds exist
  because `/login` sits outside the `[...view]` catch-all and never subscribes, so an
  unbounded buffer there would grow forever and later dump stale frames into a fresh
  page.

Not touched: `attachRun`/`detachRun`/`refreshLiveRuns` semantics, the T11 fast path,
and the `sendCommand` streaming path (it calls the same `fanout`, so a Concierge
frame dispatched during a remount is now recovered too — same seam, no extra code).

## Residual, not closed by this card

ISS-220's analyzer pass marked one link **INFERRED**: that the specific frames lost
in the 2/3 reproduced cycles fell inside this subscribe gap rather than some other
cause. This card fixes the structurally confirmed gap and the test proves the seam;
whether the live symptom clears on a real cold-start cycle is the verification pass's
call. If it does not, the next place to look is the ordering between this replay and
the T11 durable snapshot (`page.tsx:3350-3360`), which applies its frames after the
replay resolves.

The run's own `status` field never flipping from `cancelled` to `failed` after a
resumed pipeline fails is a separate backend defect, tracked by the register's
`BUG-20260828-085830-runs-cancelled` entry — untouched here.
