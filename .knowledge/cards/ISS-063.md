# ISS-063

## Title

The violet "Spec Revision Cycle N" banner (FIX-163) is LIVE-SESSION-ONLY state and never back-fills from durable events, so reopening a run that was revised shows no sign a revision ever happened. dashboard/page.tsx:357 holds specRevisionCount in useState(0) and :907-912 increments it only when revisionCycleArmedRef is armed by the FE's own click AND a matching live event arrives. Same invisibility family as FIX-217: a revision occurred and the screen does not say so.

## Source / evidence

Observed during the live Playwright pass on run d5dbc9f2 (banner ABSENT on a fresh page load of a mid-revision run); mechanism confirmed by reading the FE, not inferred.
