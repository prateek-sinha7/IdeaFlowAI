# ISS-069

## Title

**FIX-REGISTER drift — the ISS-066 defect, in the other register.** 11 `FIX-NNN` ids are in active use with NO row in `.planning/FIX-REGISTER.md`: **FIX-020** (commit messages only), **FIX-128**, **FIX-129** (both: register prose mentions + cards + code), **FIX-131** (code only), **FIX-158** (all four sources), **FIX-159**, **FIX-162**, **FIX-183**, **FIX-184** (commit messages ± code), **FIX-214**, **FIX-215** (commit messages + code). Same consequence as ISS-066: a register-only "next id" lookup under-reports, and the register cannot serve as the allocator or as the index of what shipped. Backfill each row from its commit, or record why it is absent.

## Severity / Tag / Status (original row)

- Sev: minor
- Tag: test-infra
- Status: **FIXED 2026-08-12** — all 11 rows backfilled from named evidence; `check_ids.py` now exits **0** on FIX, ISS and TEST alike (first clean run). Two tool bugs were found and fixed while closing this: (1) `check_ids.py` swept **itself**, counting the real ids cited as worked examples in its own docstring, so it reported drift it had invented — it now skips its own file; (2) a genuine blind spot is now documented rather than hidden — ids written as a range or slash-list (`FIX-158..FIX-182`, `FIX-182/183/184`) contain no literal `FIX-183` token, so the tool cannot see them and a busy range still needs a manual log grep. **Honesty note on the backfill:** 4 of the 11 (FIX-020, 158, 162, and the FIX-183/184 pair's individual split) have scope that is genuinely **unrecoverable** from the surviving evidence; those rows say so explicitly rather than inventing a plausible description. The other 7 were reconstructed from a card summary, an in-code citation, or a commit subject, and each row names which.

## Source / Evidence

Found 2026-08-12 by the new `scripts/knowledge/check_ids.py` while closing ISS-066 — the check was written as ISS-066's prevention half and immediately paid for itself. Reproduce with `python3 scripts/knowledge/check_ids.py FIX` (exit 1 on drift). Spot-verified by hand that FIX-020/128/158/214 genuinely have no `\| FIX-NNN \|` row. NOTE: the same tool initially reported 32 phantom `TEST-*` drift rows; that was a real false positive caused by two unrelated TEST namespaces (padded `TEST-001` in `FIX-TEST-REGISTER.md` vs unpadded QA-case ids `REQ-TEST-45` / `REQ-TEST-2-2` in `.knowledge/cards/`) and is now fixed in the tool — do not re-file it.
