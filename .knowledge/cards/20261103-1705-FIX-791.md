## What was wrong
Gap 5 of the workflow-gaps review. `frontend/src/types/index.ts::WorkflowType` was a literal union of 21 strings, while
the backend discovers 39 manifests. 19 manifest ids were not in the union (`chat`, the `ex_*` examples, `reverse_engineer`,
`sample_*`, `sc001-test-fixture`), and `migration` was in the union with no manifest. Runtime was already data-driven, so
dynamic paths cast catalog ids with `as WorkflowType`. The cost was compile-time only, with one latent runtime hazard:
`Sidebar.tsx` indexed `TYPE_CONFIG[run.type]`, which returns `undefined` for a manifest-only type and throws on `.icon`.
## What changed
- `types/index.ts`: the union is renamed `KnownWorkflowType`; `WorkflowType` is `KnownWorkflowType | (string & Record<never, never>)`
  so literals keep autocomplete; `isKnownWorkflowType(value)` is a runtime guard.
- `Sidebar.tsx`: `TYPE_CONFIG` is `Record<KnownWorkflowType, TypeConfig>`; `typeConfigFor()` falls back to the "Custom"
  styling with the id as the label.
- Removed the non-test `as WorkflowType` casts that became no-ops: `page.tsx` (1), `HomeLaunchGrid.tsx` (1),
  `WorkflowHistory.tsx` (4), `DashboardLayout.tsx` (about 19, plus one `as WorkflowType[]`), `PreviewPanel.tsx` (1),
  `ComposerPage.tsx` (1), `lib/api.ts` (1).
- New `workflowType.test.ts` (6 tests) and a `Sidebar.test.tsx` case for a manifest-discovered type.
## Left as it was
- Existing test-file casts still compile and were not touched.
- The comment in `IdeaInputPage.designPicker.test.tsx` that says the union is exhaustive is now out of date.
- Other `Record<string, ...>` lookups keyed by workflow type outside `Sidebar` were not audited one by one. `tsc` passes
  and no other `Record<WorkflowType, ...>` exists in non-test source.
