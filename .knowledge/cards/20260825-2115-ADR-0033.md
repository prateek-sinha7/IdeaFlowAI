## Decision

Spec 016 (`016-workflow-overrides`). A user may run a built-in workflow with
their own step list. The binding is a database row, not an edit to the manifest
on disk.

## Why a row, not a fork

Forking the manifest makes "go back to the built-in" impossible to express, and
makes every upstream change to the shipped workflow invisible to the user who
forked it. A row keyed on `overrides_pipeline_type` with an `override_enabled`
flag means the built-in is always one toggle away, and the parts an override does
NOT supply — deliverable strategy, clarify config, entitlements — keep tracking
the shipped manifest.

## Known-open by design

The spec was written retroactively against what shipped and records four gaps as
OPEN rather than pretending they were closed. Two are load-bearing here:
`base_version` is inert, and the compiler and `presort_specs` validate different
things, so a saved override can be displayed and still fail to launch. Both are
recorded rather than fixed because the spec closed on what was built.
