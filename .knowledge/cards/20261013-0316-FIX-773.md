## Related

**Referenced by:** [ISS-1142](20261013-0316-ISS-1142.md)

<!-- /RELATED -->

The backend offline CI log reported 100 deterministic failures. Root causes split between stale tests and production regressions introduced by an incomplete merge: the trigger launch port was not registered, triggered saved workflows dropped authored launch state, a capability imported the app layer directly, run-row projection used one mutable agent slot, legacy usage was persisted with zero cost, resumed attempts reused terminal in-memory state, revision errors were dropped, and spec-022 routing/counts were incomplete.

The repair restored the app-side trigger adapter with hooks, skills, model overrides, and durable manifest identity; the `KernelServices.materialise_html` boundary; keyed per-agent/task persistence with usage-v2 normalization and legacy repricing; resume attempt state reset; revision error persistence; and manifest-driven revision counts/routing. Stale workspace visibility, HTML support, token semantics, terminal harness, lineage, and manifest parity tests were aligned with current contracts.

ADR-0054 received explicit approval after its production behavior, user-visible
impact, blast radius, and behavior-preserving alternative were presented through
the workflow approval gate. The compiler now gives saved `db` workflows their own
bounded trust level: saved-safe capability opt-ins and fan-out budgets remain
available, while host-reaching grants, privileged gates, and over-ceiling limits
fail closed. Every compiled step is stamped with its manifest trust for the runtime
security gate's second-line check.
