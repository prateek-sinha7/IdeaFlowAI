# ISS-903

Found while fixing QA-KB-042 ([FIX-690](20261001-2145-FIX-690.md)). It was not fixed there, for two reasons:
the guard belongs to the test-writer/verifier, and the drifted cites below are not in either analysis card's
inventory. Line numbers in this card are for locating only.

## 1. The guard cannot see most cite shapes

`backend/tests/unit/test_run_shutdown_stale_citations.py` defines the regression guard as:

```python
_LINE_CITE = re.compile(
    r"\b(?:run_commands|engine|model_factory|run_engine|run_stream|run_shutdown|"
    r"authz|websocket|checkpointer|docker-compose)\.(?:py|yml):\d+"
)
```

The guard only matches a listed module name, then `.py`/`.yml`, then a colon with no space. The
[ISS-104](20260812-1312-ISS-104.md) analysis asked for a stricter lint: "no `name.ext:NNN` or bare `:NNNN` cite".
Run against the pre-edit snapshots of the scanned files, this guard missed 15 stale cite lines that FIX-690
converted. All 15 are listed in `evidence/qa-kb-042/09-fix-guard-blind-spots.txt`:

- bare continuations: `run_shutdown.py` `at :1356`, `at :1347`, `(:2514/:3621) ... (:5012)`, ``:2473``;
  `run_engine.py` `at :1734`, `at :1775`, `:1688`, `:2247`; `run_stream.py` `:328-330`
- a space before the colon: `run_engine.py` `(run_commands.py :399/:421, ...; engine.py :5372/:5388, ...)`
- modules outside the list: `config.py` `server.py:326-330`; `run_engine.py` `tests/unit/test_rest_resume.py:260`;
  `run_stream.py` `gate_pendency.py:53-62`

Consequence: the guard passes today because FIX-690 converted these by hand. Someone could add
`registers the queue at :2045` to one of these files tomorrow and the guard would stay green.

Disposition: open. A possible fix is to widen `_LINE_CITE` to any `[\w./-]+\.(py|yml|sh) ?:\d+` or a bare
`(?<![\w/.]):\d{3,}` in the scanned files (evidence 09 uses that pattern). The test-writer or verifier owns that
change. The fixer may not edit the test.

### Root cause (CONFIRMED)

One mechanism: the guard decides what a cite looks like from a closed list, one physical line at a time, over a hand-listed set of files.

1. `_LINE_CITE` (`backend/tests/unit/test_run_shutdown_stale_citations.py:25-28`) is ten module basenames (`run_commands|engine|model_factory|run_engine|run_stream|run_shutdown|authz|websocket|checkpointer|docker-compose`), then `.py` or `.yml`, then `:` and digits with no whitespace. Everything else is invisible: other modules (`main.py`, `server.py`, `config.py`, `h11_impl.py`, `gate_pendency.py`), `.sh` files, a space before the colon, a bare `:NNNN`. A longer basename escapes too: in `test_run_stream_pool_leak.py:196`, `run_stream` is followed by `_pool_leak.py` rather than `.py`, and `\b` fails inside `test_run_stream`. On a corpus of 15 real cite shapes the current pattern flags 1 (`docker-compose.yml:138`); the sketch's pattern flags 12; a general-extension variant flags 14 (`evidence/qa-kb-076/02`).
2. `_SCANNED` is six hand-listed paths (`:17-24`), and the scan is `for n, line in enumerate(path.read_text().splitlines(), 1)` (`:39`).
3. The guard is green over a dirty file. At HEAD `pytest tests/unit/test_run_shutdown_stale_citations.py` gives `2 passed`. The sketch's pattern finds 6 cites on 6 lines of `test_shutdown_reachability.py`; the current `_LINE_CITE` finds 0 across all six files (`evidence/qa-kb-076/01`, `03`).
4. That is why the residue exists. [FIX-690](20261001-2145-FIX-690.md) converted only "the cites the cards named or the guard flagged", and the guard flagged none of these. The two wrong test paths (docstring lines 1 and 3) come from the 2026-09-22 reorganisation `09270703b`, which renamed `tests/unit/test_shutdown_reachability.py` to `tests/unit/services/` (R099) and `tests/unit/test_run_shutdown.py` to `tests/unit/app/` (R100). A path written in text does not follow a move.

### Where the fix belongs

There is no application code in this bug. Two edits, both under `backend/tests/unit/`, with no `app.*` or `agents.*` import touched.

1. **One regex constant.** `_LINE_CITE` has exactly one definition and one consumer (`:25`, `:40`; `grep -rnE '_LINE_CITE|_SCANNED' backend tests tools ci`, `evidence/qa-kb-076/03`). Widening it there is the whole guard-side fix. Recommended shape: any path-like token, a general extension class, an optional space, a colon, digits; or a bare `:NNN` not glued to a word, slash or dot, which is `[\w./-]+\.[A-Za-z]{1,5} ?:\d+|(?<![\w/.]):\d{3,}`. The sketch's `py|yml|sh` list gives the same 6 hits on today's files; the general class also catches `workflow.yaml:12` and `pyproject.toml:174` ([ISS-1082](20261007-2216-ISS-1082.md)). On the corpus's negatives (`localhost:3000`, `0.0.0.0:8000`, `12:30:45`, `ISS-104: see FIX-690`) it flags nothing.
2. **Comments in `test_shutdown_reachability.py`**, per the inventory above. Comments and docstring only; no assertion touched.

Convert lines 5-11 as a unit: the widened pattern flags lines 6, 8 and 10, and the wrapped `292` on line 11 is the same sentence. Converting to symbols matches [FIX-690](20261001-2145-FIX-690.md)'s locked constraint (never `file:line`) and needs no extra guard logic. The alternative, exempting third-party paths in the guard, adds a rule to keep. Either is the fixer's call, not a product decision.
