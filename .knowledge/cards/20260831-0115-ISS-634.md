## Why this ranks above the bigger clusters

Every one asserts that content survives the sanitizer unchanged. A sanitizer
that alters a CLEAN stream is dropping or rewriting text a user is reading —
and the last guard is explicitly about **content loss at flush**. Four tests is
a small number; the failure mode is not.
