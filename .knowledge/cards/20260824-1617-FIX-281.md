Opening a built-in workflow on the composer canvas produced steps with no prompts, and
saving it would have persisted them that way.

## Root cause

`WorkflowDetail.steps` is the COMPILED projection: `agent_id`, `name`, `role`, `order`,
`strategy`, `gates`, `validators`, `compaction`, `task_source`, `skills`. It carries no
`prompt`, no `tools`, no `instance_id`, no `depends_on` and no `route` — everything an
editor needs. There was no other endpoint exposing them.

## Fix

`manifest_steps: list[dict] | None` added to `WorkflowDetail`, passing
`WorkflowManifest.steps` through verbatim. Already parsed and validated by
`load_manifest`, so this adds no new derivation and no new trust surface — it is the same
data the compiler consumes.

This is what makes the canvas able to show a step's prompt, its tool grants, and its
conditional-gate branches, and what `ComposerPage.initialManifestSteps` is fed from.
