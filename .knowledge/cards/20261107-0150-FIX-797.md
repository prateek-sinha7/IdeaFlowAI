## Root causes
1. Grayscale or indexed Figma exports made Bedrock ConverseStream reject the vision request.
2. The ingestor read all N frames sequentially in one conversation; at roughly 10 images Bedrock returned 400 "The model
   rejected this request".
3. nginx returned 413 on frame uploads because no `location` block covered the endpoint.

## Evidence level
Grants and function located on 2026-11-07, and the three test files exist at the paths above. They were not re-run. The
nginx change is not located in this card.
