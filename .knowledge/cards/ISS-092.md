# ISS-092

## Title

A single Concierge chat question can feed the model up to 2.3M tokens, and not one of them is counted or capped. Two compounding facts, both verified 2026-08-12. (1) Uncounted: app/agents/chat/concierge.py:365 drains its runner stream with if event["type"] != "chunk": continue, so every usage event is discarded. Nothing anywhere records a Concierge token — its spend is not merely under-reported, it is *unmeasurable from the data*. (2) Unbounded input: concierge.py:434 calls scoped_store.read_events(run_id, 0) and agents/authz.py:315-327 applies no LIMIT and no type filter, projecting every row including 200k+ agent_chunk payloads. Measured payload available at each recorded question (6 conversations, 15 chat_message events in dev.db): fa66227a@1322 ≈53.6k tok · caa5d175@10519 ≈385.7k · a7dba362@7721 ≈267.3k · 0a27b397@12123 ≈2,306,800 tok. On a $0.34 user_stories run a single 205k-token Concierge read is ~60% of the run's entire input. The replies are coherent multi-turn answers, so the calls succeeded — what the model actually received is simply unknowable. Same class, same row: the handoff pipeline is 100% invisible — handoff_pipeline.py:403/:494/:514 pass no usage_sink (the seams exist, unwired) and handoff_sessions has no token column at all; handoff/coder.py:235 also drops its usage. Why this was NOT bundled into FIX-230: converse runs from a REST handler (run_commands.py:1442) outside execute(), often after the run is terminal, so aux_token_usage is out of scope AND out of lifetime. Wiring it needs an answer to a product question — *should user-initiated chat spend appear in the run's headline cost, or as a separate line?* — and the handoff half needs a cost surface that does not exist. Both are features, not bug fixes.

## Source / evidence

Split out of ISS-033 during quick-260812-9tq, whose part A (FIX-230) closed the two measurable, safely-foldable sites. Evidence: read-only queries against the live backend/dev.db + the cited file:line. The rejected shortcut, recorded so it is not re-proposed: estimating Concierge spend from len(chat_reply) (or the fix-loop from a fudge factor) puts a fabricated number where a real one is one event-branch away — and would make ISS-034's "saved $Y" a fiction built on a fiction. If a token was not observed it must be reported as unmeasured, never invented. Independent of accounting, the uncapped read_events is also a latent context-blowout and latency risk.
