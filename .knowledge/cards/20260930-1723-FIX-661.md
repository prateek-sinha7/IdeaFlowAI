## Bug

The Backlog Planner built its own list of coverage items by paging the nine model files. In run 3976f8ce it read endpoints.json only to about line 3000: API-34..API-76 were in no epic, its own check compared against its own short list and passed, and the run failed at its last step at 83% coverage.

## Fix

- `us-json` writes `build/coverage-items.json`, the complete list (id, type, title, provenance), and the Planner works from it.
- New `backlog_plan_coverage` validator on the Planner step (`gates: [validation]`, `fix: {max_attempts: 2}`): every coverage item must be in an epic's `covers` or in `unassigned`, and every covered item must be named by a slice task in `build/story-tasks.json`. A gap re-runs the Planner with the missing ids, then fails the run at this step, so a resume starts there.
- Final Assemble never fails on coverage; it writes `gaps.md` as the last PDF chapter. `build-order.md` gains feature- and story-level dependency maps.
- Engine: resume re-runs a step whose validation was exhausted.
