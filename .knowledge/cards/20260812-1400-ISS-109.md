# ISS-109

## Title

**`validator_result` and `gate_passed` have ZERO producers in non-test backend code, so `validationIssues` may be dead on the wire and `AgentDetailPanel`'s `checksPassed === true` may be structurally unreachable.** `gate_blocked` has 3 real producers and sets `validationPassed: false`; nothing emits the two frames that would set it `true` or populate `validationIssues` (`useWorkflow.ts:986-1021`). `AgentDetailPanel.tsx:144-145` reads `agent.validationIssues?.length` and `agent.validationPassed === true && ...`. The ISS-082 fix repairs the `validator_result` accumulator anyway, for consistency and so the family rule holds uniformly — but if the frame has no producer, the surface is DEAD rather than merely un-versioned, which changes what ISS-087 should do. Needs its own investigation: either wire the producer or delete the consumer (INV-12), not both.
