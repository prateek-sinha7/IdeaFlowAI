## Change

Spec: `specs/022-prototype-v2-revisions/`.

- Two composed manifests: `prototype_v2_revision` (Fast: analyzer, fixer, validator) and `prototype_v2_feature_revision` (Depth: analyzer, change analyst, planner, builder, validator). `REVISION_BASE_MAP` maps both to `prototype_v2`.
- `run_commands.py`: `create_revision` and the Concierge path classify a `prototype_v2_output` request with `_classify_revision_tier` and route it through `_V2_REVISION_TIER_TARGET_MAP` (small -> Fast; large or feature -> Depth). On a v2 parent, a model-supplied v1 target is rewritten to `prototype_v2_output`.
- `_mint_revision_row` counts the compiled plan (`_compiled_step_count`) when a composed manifest has no AGENT.md roster.
- Entitlements on both sides gain the two types.
- Frontend: the v2 revision types, sidebar entries and prototype-family checks; `DashboardLayout.handleRevisePrototypeV2` posts Revise on a v2 run to the revisions endpoint, while v1 keeps the diff path.

## Open

Both manifests are marked TEMP (`user_launchable: true`, `is_beta: false`) so they can be launched for testing. They go back before release.
