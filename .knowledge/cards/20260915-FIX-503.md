## What happened

The `prototype-revision-analyzer` AGENT.md declared `max_tokens: 4096`. For
non-trivial prototypes, the revision analysis payload exceeds that limit,
causing the model to truncate its output mid-response. Downstream agents
(planner, revision builder) then worked from an incomplete specification.

## Fix

Raised `max_tokens` to `32768` in
`backend/agents/prompts/prototype-revision-analyzer/AGENT.md`.
