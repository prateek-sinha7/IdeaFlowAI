## Related

## What was wrong

Before-human gates were attributing their input artifact (produced by the 
preceding step) to the gate step itself. This made the gate row appear to 
produce output, when in fact the preceding step did.

Gates that review output from the preceding step should display that output 
attributed to the step that produced it, not to the gate. The gate step should 
show only gate events (gate_ready, gate_passed, etc.), not the artifact.

## The fix (commit 1e329e47b)

Landed in commit `1e329e47b` with the files listed under `globs`; the tests under `verification.test_files` pin the behaviour.
