### Root cause (one mechanism)

The live e2e tier (`tests/integration/e2e`, 25 suites) re-types facts about the app as literals, in the shared fixture and locator layer and in the suite files: a request body, UI strings, tab names, bug-status xfail markers, database ids. Nothing couples a literal to the thing it copies, and nothing runs the tier when the app changes. Five intentional commits changed the facts; each updated its unit-tier twin and none touched an e2e file. The drift sat until validate-bugs ran all 25 suites on 2026-10-09.
