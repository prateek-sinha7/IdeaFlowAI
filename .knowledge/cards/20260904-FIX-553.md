## Related

## What was wrong

Non-fatal capability violations (e.g., validators emitting non-CRITICAL findings) 
were rendering as red "failed" status, which incorrectly suggests the run is 
broken. There was no amber status for "completed with warnings" — either green 
(passed) or red (failed).

Runs that complete with validator warnings or non-fatal findings should display 
as amber "completed with warnings" to distinguish them from both successful runs 
(green) and genuinely failed runs (red).

## The fix (commit 1e329e47b)

Landed in commit `1e329e47b` with the files listed under `globs`; the tests under `verification.test_files` pin the behaviour.
