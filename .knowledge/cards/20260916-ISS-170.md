# ISS-170

## What was found

`deploy.yml` contains two explicit temporary reductions in deployment protection:

1. The `gate` reusable-workflow job is skipped when the resolved environment is `dev`; the build
   and deploy conditions then accept either a successful gate or `dev`.
2. Backend and frontend image scans still run by digest, but HIGH/CRITICAL findings are converted
   to warning annotations and deployment continues.

The workflow comments state both exceptions are temporary. The dev bypass exists because
pre-existing CI debt blocks unrelated deployments. The image-scan exception exists because known
findings include fixable dependency pins and findings awaiting an upstream/base-image decision.

## Risk

A dev deployment can proceed without the normal CI evidence gate, and a HIGH or CRITICAL image
finding can currently reach the target environment. The two exceptions preserve visibility and
limit the environment scope where stated, but they are intentionally weaker than the normal
release posture.

## Required resolution

- Restore the normal dev gate after the cited CI debt is remediated by removing the conditional
  skip and the matching build/deploy allowances.
- Restore blocking digest scans after the pinnable findings are fixed and every remaining finding
  has an explicit reviewed disposition, using `trivy image --severity HIGH,CRITICAL --exit-code 1`.
- Keep the restoration covered by the workflow-contract suite so a future maintenance edit cannot
  silently preserve or widen the exception.
