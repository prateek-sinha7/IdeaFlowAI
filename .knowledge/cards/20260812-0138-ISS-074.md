# ISS-074

## Title

**The LIVE harness's review-gate wrapper cannot accept the gate's own arguments — every live gated drive raises TypeError.** `backend/tests/agents/live_harness.py:672` defines `_auto_resume_review_gate(pipeline_run_id, agent_id, agent_name, output)` and installs it over `engine._run_review_gate` unconditionally at `:683`, but the engine's three inline call sites pass `redoable=`, `update_specs_eligible=`, `artifact_kind=`, `cancel_event=` (and, since FIX-220, `revision_cycle=` / `revision_in_flight=`). The wrapper also forwards only the same four positionally to `_orig_review_gate`, so even a widened signature would drop them. Pre-existing since REDO-GATE added `redoable`; it has rotted unnoticed because the live harness is SSO/Bedrock-gated and never runs in the offline sweeps. Fix is two tokens — `**kwargs` on the def and on the forwarded call — but it is UNVERIFIABLE offline, which is why it was filed rather than patched. Exactly the class of rot that FIX-218 already had to repair in four `_fake_gate` stubs and FIX-220 in three `_gate` stubs; a signature-drift guard over the stubs would end the family.
