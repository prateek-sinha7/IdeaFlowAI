One `prototype_revision` manifest became three, chosen by a pre-classification step.

## The decision

`revision_analyzer` runs first, returns a tier and a solution plan, and the tier routes to
`prototype_revision`, `prototype_large_revision`, or `prototype_feature_revision`.

## Why this shape

The three manifests **share agents** rather than duplicating them — which is what forced
`pipeline_type` to accept a list (see the sibling ADR). `prototype-build` and
`prototype-validate` each belong to three pipelines from one AGENT.md.

The analyzer's output reaches the agent through one additive context block appended only
when `ectx.analyzer_solution` is non-empty. That dormancy is what keeps the five existing
characterization goldens byte-identical without re-baselining.
