## Symptom

```
FAILED …test_iss592… - [XPASS(strict)] ISS-592 unfixed
FAILED …test_iss593… - [XPASS(strict)] ISS-593 unfixed
FAILED …test_iss594… - [XPASS(strict)] ISS-594 unfixed

        AssertionError: Escape now closes the inspect dialog — the spec's
        S-15-01 can be asserted in full, and S-20-01 gains a row
```

**These are wins, not breakages.** A strict XPASS means a test pinned to
known-broken behaviour passed: the bug is gone. The four ISS cards are already
`resolved` and correct; only their markers are stale.

## What to do

The one-line change is removing `@pytest.mark.xfail(reason="… unfixed",
strict=True)`. **The work is proving the fix is real** — a strict XPASS is a
claim, not proof, and a test can drift into passing vacuously. Reproduce each
original bug by hand, confirm it no longer happens, then remove the marker.

For S-15-01, also update the spec: drop `@pytest.mark.defect`, assert the
scenario in full, and add the inspect dialog as a row in S-20-01 ("Escape
closes any overlay") in `screens/20-keyboard-and-navigation`.

Markers live at `test_iss592…:28`, `test_iss593…:26`, `test_iss594…:28`,
`test_settings.py:440`.
