## What was wrong

`frontend/src/app/login/page.tsx:303-317` retained a hand-rolled
`type="password"` `<input>` with no reveal toggle — the single field left out of
FIX-398 because it had a different wrapper shape (Lock icon at `left-3.5`).

## What changed

`frontend/src/app/login/page.tsx` only:

- Replaced the bare `<input type="password" ...>` with `<PasswordInput ...>` (already
  imported in the file).
- Kept the outer `input-focus relative` wrapper and the `<Lock>` icon — the Lock sits
  in the outer wrapper at `z-10 pointer-events-none`; `PasswordInput`'s own inner
  `relative` wrapper holds the eye-toggle on the right. The padding `pl-10` on the
  input ensures the text clears the Lock icon; `PasswordInput` appends `pr-10` for
  the toggle.
