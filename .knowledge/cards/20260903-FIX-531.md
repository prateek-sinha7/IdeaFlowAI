## Related

## What was wrong

`degraded` was given a softer word than `failed` on every surface — "Issues" in
the history pill, "Completed with issues" in the chat terminal card. A run that
had lost an agent read as a qualified success.

## The fix (commit 3e6fcb93c)

The status stays distinct in the database and in the API; only the wording
changes. The history pill, the lane breadcrumb and the chat terminal card all
read "Failed", in the failed colour.
