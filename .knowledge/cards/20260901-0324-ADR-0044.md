## Related

## Decision

A composed workflow can now turn uploaded documents into a deliverable, which
needs three things decided together (commit 127b20a3d):

1. **Tool packs are declared per step and cap-intersected.** `ToolPermissions.
   tool_sets` names packs; `permission_caps.py` intersects them with the
   caller's cap. The untrusted allow-list — what a user-authored step may
   declare — is `["file_extract", "playwright"]` and nothing else.
2. **Uploads are staged, then ingested before dispatch.** `POST /api/runs/
   uploads/stage` returns ids; launch accepts `staged_upload_ids` and ingests
   the files into the run workspace before the first agent runs, so readers
   never race the upload.
3. **The Composer exposes packs as named toggles** (Tools tab) that persist
   through save/reopen, alongside fan-out selections that round-trip instead of
   degrading to a plain group.

## Why

The trust wall (ADR-0027) stays intact: nothing here widens what a user can
grant, it only lets a step choose among already-trusted packs. Staging before
dispatch is what makes "start with documents attached" deterministic.

## Consequences

`backend/skills/global/file-extraction/SKILL.md` documents the pack for agents.
Any new pack must be added to the cap side first; adding it to the UI alone
does nothing (intersection).
