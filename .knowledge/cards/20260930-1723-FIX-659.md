## Bug

Story Writers read "6 / 1 wave": its 5 finished workers plus the Readers' 1, over the Readers' wave total. `AgentDetailPanel` handed the run's waves, which span every wave step, to `WaveWorkersBlock`.

## Fix

`waveGroups` is filtered to `w.step === agent.id` (waves with no step are kept).
