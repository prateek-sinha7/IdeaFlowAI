# ADR-0004

## Decision

In the context of two competing orderings — the resolver's topo-sort over agent-declared
produces/consumes and the compiler's topo-sort over manifest-declared depends_on —
facing four instances of one template that share one contract-free AGENT.md and
therefore cannot be ordered by contracts at all, we decided declared order wins whenever
the compiled plan carries a depends_on edge, to achieve a runnable composed workflow
without touching the resolver, accepting that one such edge currently flips ordering for
the entire plan.

**Area:** engine, workflow
