# ISS-123

## Title

**COUNT + REASONING CORRECTED 2026-08-12 (FIX-246). The damage is 15 destroyed engine events across 9 runs, NOT ~29 across 10** — `agent_chunk` 11, `questionnaire_complete` 2, `review_gate_approved` 1, `pipeline_cancelled` 1. The 29 counted seq-STEALING app rows, which is an upper bound, not the loss: a steal destroys an engine event only when the engine counter actually WALKS onto it, and 12 steals hit runs with no live drive on that seq while 2 were absorbed by a narrator-card counter jump. Re-derived independently by `backend/scripts/audit_lost_run_events.py` (read-only, `mode=ro`, zero write verbs). **The stated reason for refusing back-fill was half wrong and is corrected:** "a dropped row has no provenance to reconstruct from" is FALSE for 11 of the 15 — they are `agent_chunk` fragments and `artifact_refs.content` is an independent authoritative record of the same text; the audit sizes each hole against it and reproduces the lost strings exactly by length (1 char = the document`s leading `#`, 4, 14, 26, 27). **The correct reason is narrower and stronger: the CONTENT is recoverable, the IDENTITY is not.** `event_id` is the key the whole replay contract rests on (`replayed_event_ids`, `append_event_next_seq` idempotency, the FE `seenRef`), so a back-filled row must mint an `event_id` that never existed — making the log assert the engine emitted a frame it did not, with a de-dup key no live frame can match. The four structural losses are unrecoverable outright: `workflow_clarifications` is EMPTY corpus-wide and `gate_events` records NO approve outcome for any run. **User impact: exactly 1 run was user-visible-broken (`808612bf`) and that symptom is now fixed by FIX-245 (ISS-126); the other 8 are silently lossy** (a reopened transcript short by 1–27 characters of one agent`s text; the DELIVERABLE is unaffected — Preview/Files read `artifact_refs`/`output`). **FIX-240 stops NEW losses; it cannot recover the rows already destroyed.** The pre-fix defect ran for as long as the chat lane has shared the engine's seq space, and a dropped `run_events` row has no provenance to reconstruct from. Corpus-wide upper bound measured read-only over `backend/dev.db`: **29 seq-stealing app-layer rows across 10 runs** (`chat_message` 19, concierge `chat_reply` 8, `chat_usage` 2) against 233,325 rows in 16 runs — i.e. up to **29 silently destroyed engine events**. Three identified by name: `questionnaire_complete` in runs `808612bf` and `f36ab26c`, one `pipeline_cancelled` (`808612bf`), and an `agent_chunk` in `ec7fb984` (seq 3581). Consumers that silently mis-read a holed log: SSE durable replay, `derive_open_gate`, `_reconcile_terminal_status` (a dropped terminal makes a user-resume fall through to `new_status="failed"`), `_apply_terminal_output_columns`, the conversation context provider, the Concierge, and `GET /runs/{id}/events`. **Deliberately NOT fixed:** back-filling synthetic terminal rows was considered and REJECTED — fabricating durable events with no `event_id` provenance is worse than the hole, and it would make the log lie about what the engine actually emitted. **Money is NOT exposed:** the startup restore scan filters on `WorkflowRun.status`, which is correct, so a holed cancelled run is never re-driven.

## Source / Evidence

Measured by the ISS-121 root-cause investigation (read-only SQL, `mode=ro`). Candidate disposition: accept as historical data loss and record it, or write a one-off read-only audit that lists affected runs so support can recognise them. **2026-08-12 (FIX-246):** shipped `backend/scripts/audit_lost_run_events.py` — a read-only forensic audit so the damage is nameable and re-measurable. It simulates the seq allocator (a gap proves nothing: the steal OCCUPIES the seq) and reports two signatures, WALKED-ONTO and ORPHAN-CARD (a narrator card whose source `event_id` has no row — a tombstone that NAMES the destroyed event). Three self-corrections are recorded in the script: post-FIX-240 runs show the same shape but lost nothing (only creation time discriminates -> `--fix240-utc`), a destroyed `review_gate_approved` sits TWO rows after its `review_gate_ready` because the narrator card is between them, and sizing a hole against the SUM of `artifact_refs` versions reported a 1-char hole as 18,718.
