## Related

## What was wrong

Event loop delays and slow requests had no visibility; when the loop froze, 
there was no diagnostic data to identify the cause.

## The fix (commit 1e329e47b)

- New watchdog module: monitors event loop lag and logs anomalies.
- Slow-request middleware logs requests exceeding a configurable threshold.
- Diagnostic only: no blocking, no altered behavior, just logging.
- Helps identify future event-loop freezes faster.

Pinned by test_watchdog.py and test_slow_request_middleware.py verifying 
diagnostic logging without operational impact.
