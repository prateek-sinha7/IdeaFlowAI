## The symptom

The Daily Activity chart read as continuous activity across a gap that had
simply been dropped. A quiet week **vanished** rather than flattening, and the
axis silently compressed — consecutive bars for non-consecutive days, with
nothing on screen saying so.

## The root cause

`daily` is a `defaultdict` populated only while walking runs. A day with no
runs therefore never became a key, never reached the client, and never got a
bar. The chart has no way to tell a missing day from a day that was never in
the range.

## What changed

The interior of the range is filled — every date between the first and last
day that carry runs gets a key. Because `daily` is a defaultdict, indexing a
filled date mints its own zeroed bucket from the factory, so no bucket shape is
duplicated by hand.

## Why the interior only

Padding out to the request window's full cutoff would invent leading days for
an account whose history starts inside the window, and `_aggregate` is not
handed the cutoff, so it could not do that correctly even if it should.
