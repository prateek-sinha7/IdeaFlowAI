# ISS-1010

Sibling of [ISS-898](20261001-2128-ISS-898.md), found by the QA-KB-071 analysis on 2026-10-02. Confidence: CONFIRMED by a mutation run on a
scratch copy; the repo test file and `page.tsx` were not edited. A validator re-run is still pending.

## Why the root cause implies it

[ISS-898](20261001-2128-ISS-898.md) is "a bare-substring anchor in a 250K-character file stopped matching what the author meant". This case
is the other half of that failure: an anchor that still matches, but matches the wrong place. `for (const frame of
durableFrames)` appears three times in `page.tsx`, and `indexOf` returns the first.

## Condition

The case `runs AFTER the durable replay loop, never before it` (test lines 75-81), read together with its neighbour
`stays inside the reopen handler, after the durable fetch` (lines 83-88):

```ts
const loopStart = pageSource.indexOf("for (const frame of durableFrames)");
const call = pageSource.indexOf("applyTerminalStatus(prev, fullRun.status)");
...
expect(call).toBeGreaterThan(loopStart);
```

The three loops in `page.tsx` on 2026-10-02:

| line | handler | its fetch |
|---|---|---|
| `:2824` | `handleSwitchToLiveRun` (`:2745`), the in-app switch | `getRunEvents(switchToken, runId)` at `:2815` |
| `:3074` | `handleSelectWorkflowRun` (`:2903`), the history reopen the ISS-126 reconcile follows | `getRunEvents(currentToken, fullRun.id)` at `:3069` |
| `:3657` | the launch-triggered remount replay of a live run | `getRunEvents(currentToken, runId)` at `:3651` |

`loopStart` is `:2824`.

## Expected

The test's own header: "ORDERING IS THE WHOLE POINT. Placed BEFORE the replay loop, the reconciliation is immediately
overwritten by the replayed `review_gate_ready`/`pipeline_start` and the bug persists — while a naive "does it call
it?" assertion still passes. So this test asserts POSITION, not just presence." Moving the reconcile ahead of the reopen
handler's replay loop must turn the lock red.

## Actual

It stays green. `call > loopStart` only proves the reconcile sits below the run-switch handler's loop. The neighbour case
adds `call > fetchAt` (`:3069`). The lines between `getRunEvents(currentToken, fullRun.id)` (`:3069`) and the reopen loop
(`:3074`) satisfy both, and that is exactly the placement the lock exists to forbid.

It has been blind since it was written. At `2b25d3d05` (2026-08-12) the first loop in `dashboard/page.tsx` was the switch
handler at `:2062`, the reopen loop was at `:2235` and the reconcile at `:2284`, and the test line was identical
(`git show 2b25d3d05:frontend/src/app/dashboard/terminalReopenReconcile.source.test.ts`, line 76).

Today the case is red, and for the wrong reason (`expected -1 to be greater than -1`, the [ISS-898](20261001-2128-ISS-898.md) anchor). Once
[ISS-898](20261001-2128-ISS-898.md) is re-anchored it goes green while still blind. Do not read that green as proof of ordering.

## Evidence

- `evidence/qa-kb-071/02-analyze-scratch-ab.out.txt` section 5: the loops sit at lines `2824, 3074, 3657`.
- Same file, section 3: the reconcile block moved to just before the reopen loop (reconcile at line 3093, reopen fetch
  3069, reopen loop 3111, first loop 2824). The re-anchored test then gives `Tests 10 passed (10)`, with no `×` line.
  The mutation is `evidence/qa-kb-071/03-analyze-mutate-reopen-block.cjs <in page.tsx> <out page.tsx>`; it writes only the
  out file, so point it at a scratch path.
- Same file, sections 4a and 4b: with the loop searched from the reopen fetch, the current page gives
  `Tests 10 passed (10)` and the mutated page gives `Tests 1 failed | 9 passed (10)`, failing exactly
  `runs AFTER the durable replay loop, never before it`.

## Fix direction

Same edit as [ISS-898](20261001-2128-ISS-898.md), same file. In the ordering case, compute `fetchAt` (the reopen handler's
`const durableFrames = await getRunEvents(currentToken, fullRun.id);`), assert it is above -1, and search the loop from
it: `pageSource.indexOf("for (const frame of durableFrames)", fetchAt)`. The mutation in section 3 is the negative
control to keep: with the fix in place it must turn the case red. No change to `page.tsx`.

## Resolution — FIX-731 (2026-10-07)

Resolved by [FIX-731](20261007-1908-FIX-731.md). The ordering case computes the reopen `fetchAt`, asserts it is above -1,
and searches `indexOf("for (const frame of durableFrames)", fetchAt)` (`terminalReopenReconcile.source.test.ts:81-84`).
The negative control was re-run on scratch copies. The fixed lock gives 10 of 10 on the current `page.tsx`. On the
`03-analyze-mutate-reopen-block.cjs` copy it gives 1 failed | 9 passed, and the one failure is this case
(`evidence/qa-kb-071/fix-06`). The meta-lock's ISS-1010 `it.fails` case now fails with "Expect test to fail". The
marker is left for the 6-verifier.
