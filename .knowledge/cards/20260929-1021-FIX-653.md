## Change

- Model chip on wave members, fan-out children and sub-agent rows. A wave/fan-out
  step header borrows its first member's model — its own `agent_start` /
  `agent_complete` carry no `model_id`.
- Wave steps show their members' token sums (live while running) in the step
  card, spine, wave sections and run totals. A wave-repair re-run adds to the
  first attempt's usage instead of replacing it.
- Token summary, chat widget and analytics show the cache read/write split.
