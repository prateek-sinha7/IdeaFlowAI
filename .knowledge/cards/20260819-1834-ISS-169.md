## Reasoning/Output live display didn't match real provider streaming order or scroll expectations

**Follow-up to [ISS-166](20260819-1726-ISS-166.md)/[FIX-265](20260819-1726-FIX-265.md).** That fix made the live Output card render
whenever the agent was running, alongside Reasoning if present — correct in that it no
longer hid Output, but it modeled Reasoning and Output as independently concurrent.
In practice, extended-thinking providers always finish their entire thinking block
before the text block starts — Reasoning and Output are sequential phases of one
turn, not two parallel streams. Two further gaps surfaced from live use:

1. **No visual handoff between phases.** Both cards showed simultaneously and stayed
   full-size for the whole turn, growing the panel and leaving no signal that
   Reasoning had finished and Output was now the active one.
2. **Scroll always snapped to bottom.** `ReasoningCard`'s auto-scroll effect
   unconditionally forced `scrollTop = scrollHeight` on every text update while live —
   scrolling up to re-read earlier text during a long stream was immediately undone by
   the next token.
3. **Inconsistent spacing.** `ReasoningCard`'s outer wrapper carried no margin, unlike
   every sibling card in the same list (`ValidationResultCard`, `RevisionInstructionCard`,
   etc., all `mb-3`), so it sat flush against neighboring elements.

**Fix:** [FIX-267](20260819-1834-FIX-267.md) reworks the transition and scroll behavior in
`AgentDetailPanel.tsx` to match how the stream actually behaves and how a reader
expects to follow it.
