## Related

## What was wrong

Composed workflows could not begin with documents already attached. Users had to
launch the workflow first, then upload files, creating a race condition and a poor UX
when documents were needed before the run's first step could proceed.

## The fix (commit 127b20a3d)

- Add `staged_upload_ids: list[str] | None = None` to LaunchCommand.
- After minting the run, call `ingest_staged_uploads()` to move documents from
  staging into the run's .uploads/ directory (and all sidecars/manifest.json).
- Ingest happens BEFORE the engine driver spawns, so no race on file_info()/read.
- Failures are logged (via `logger.exception`) but do not fail the launch; the
  minted run must not be stranded due to an upload problem.
