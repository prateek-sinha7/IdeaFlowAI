# ISS-105

## Title

lifespan.shutdown() is entered BEFORE the SSE request generators finish unwinding, so shutdown_run_infrastructure() snapshots _PIPELINE_QUEUES / _PUMP_TASKS while stream teardown is still in flight. uvicorn/server.py:287-292 cancels server_state.tasks when the graceful window expires and then calls await self.lifespan.shutdown() without awaiting the cancelled tasks. Reproduced twice on the throwaway probe: lifespan_shutdown_entered is stamped ~0.4 ms BEFORE sse_generator_finally (t=1786531398.0193348 vs 1786531398.019713, and again at 1786531538.125244 vs 1786531538.126372). In the real app the SSE generator's finally (run_stream.py:413-419) only logs, so no harm is expected — but the full subscriber-detach path has NOT been traced under concurrent teardown, and step 2/2b of shutdown_run_infrastructure reason about exactly those registries. Fix shape: either await the cancelled connection tasks before the lifespan (not ours to change — it is uvicorn), or make the step 2/2b snapshot tolerant of concurrent mutation and assert that tolerance in a test.

## Source / evidence

Observed during quick-260812-hsx in the FIX-234 flag experiments (two independent runs, marker timestamps above). Not chased further because it is out of ISS-088's scope and the observed impact is nil; recorded so the next reader does not have to re-derive the ordering.
