## What was wrong
Gap 1 of the workflow-gaps review (`.agents/tasks/workflow-gaps-verification.md`, classified REAL).
`WorkflowManifest.capabilities` parses and validates `{"internet": true}` (`backend/agents/workflows/manifest.py`),
and `CompiledWorkflow.capabilities` exists (`backend/agents/workflows/plan.py`). The engine copies it into
`ectx.compiled_capabilities` and threads it into `AgentContext`, and the factory reads `ctx.capabilities.get("internet")`.
The one production `CompiledWorkflow(...)` construction in `WorkflowCompiler.compile` had no `capabilities=` argument,
so the field was always `{}`. The Composer persisted the toggle in `manifest_json`, the launch paths
(`run_commands.py`, `compile_with_baseline`) handed it to the compiler, and the compiler dropped it.
A direct probe of a manifest with `capabilities={"internet": True}` returned `compiled.capabilities == {}` at trust `file`
and `db`.
The existing threading test (`test_capabilities_threading.py`) patched `compile_for_run` to stamp `capabilities` onto an
already compiled plan, and the manifest tests only checked parsing. Nothing covered manifest to compiler.
## What changed
- `backend/agents/workflows/compiler.py:471`: `capabilities=dict(getattr(manifest, "capabilities", {}) or {})`.
- New `test_compiler_capabilities.py` (46 tests): internet true, false and omitted, copy semantics, trust `user`,
  YAML to `load_manifest` to compile, and one case per shipped manifest (39).
- `test_capabilities_threading.py`: `via_compiler=True` helper option and
  `test_manifest_internet_capability_reaches_agent_context_via_compiler`, which drives manifest to `WorkflowCompiler` to
  `ExecutionEngine.execute()` to `create_runner` and checks `AgentContext.capabilities == {"internet": True}`.
## Shipped behaviour
Compiled all 39 manifests before and after (snapshots in `.agents/tasks/workflow-gaps/compiled-snapshot-*.json`).
`capabilities` changed for exactly 13 manifests, from `{}` to `{"internet": false}`. No manifest enables internet.
The Internet tools registered today are stubs without network egress, so no outbound traffic starts from this fix.
