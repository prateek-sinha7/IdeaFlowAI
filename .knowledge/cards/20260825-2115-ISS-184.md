Override validation covered three workflows end to end, but only two deliverable families were
genuinely proven:

| workflow | strategy | proven? |
|---|---|---|
| `ppt` | `ppt` (last-streamed) | yes — 5-slide deck rendered |
| `user_stories` | `streamed_text` (last-streamed) | yes — backlog, no leakage from the added agent |
| `prototype` | `single_file` (sandbox readback) | **no** |

The `prototype` override was deliberately shortened to `[documentation-agent,
prototype-specify]` to keep token cost down, which omits `prototype-build` — the only step that
writes `prototype.html`. `single_file` found no file and fell back to last-streamed. Correct
documented behaviour, but it means **no run has yet shown a sandbox-readback deliverable
surviving an override**.

`app_builder`, `mulesoft_to_springboot` and `dotnet_to_azure` (`serialized_sandbox`) were saved
and served correctly; `app_builder` ran 1 step to completion, the other two were cancelled as out
of scope.

**Disposition:** open. Needs one `prototype` override run that retains `prototype-build`.

## Resolution (2026-08-26)

Answered by [FIX-314](20260826-0124-FIX-314.md). `single_file` reads the sandbox
correctly and its fallback to `last_streamed` is deliberate — what decides whether it
yields the real artifact is whether the producing step was granted `tools.write_files`.
The suspicion recorded here pointed at the resolver; the resolver was never at fault.
