Three prototype agents (`prototype-plan`, `-build`, `-validate`) are reused by the tiered
revision pipelines. Before this, one agent belonged to exactly one pipeline, so reuse
would have meant duplicate AGENT.md files kept in sync by hand.

## The decision

`pipeline_type` accepts a non-empty string or a non-empty list of strings
(`loader._parse_pipeline_type`). `list_agent_ids` and the `SUPPORTED_PIPELINE_TYPES`
derivation both expand a list.

## The trap this creates

The widening has to be carried to EVERY consumer. `str | list[str]` still overlaps `str`,
so `a.pipeline_type === workflowType` typechecks and just returns false — the agent
vanishes from the roster with no error. Found at merge in `ComposerPage` and
`useAgentLibrary`, and in `AgentResponse.pipeline_type: str`, which raised a pydantic
ValidationError (HTTP 500) on `GET /agents/pipelines/prototype`.
