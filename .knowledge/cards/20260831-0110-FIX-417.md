## What changed

`test_picking_a_version_in_the_menu_writes_the_pin_into_the_url` now pins to v2
of the 5-member family `b868a6b6-ed11-4ae9-a512-6df2b1c5ad4e` — the fixture
`test_iss_607_610_611_612_base_route_revision_unreachable.py` already uses —
and picks `Version v3`, asserting the URL ends `/versions/3`. v3 is neither the
root nor the un-pinned target, so it exercises a genuine pin rewrite.

A new test, `test_picking_the_version_the_base_route_already_shows_drops_the_pin`,
asserts the other half deliberately: picking v1 lands on the bare
`/runs/{root}`. Without it the shortcut is behaviour nothing guards, and the
next person reading the ISS-296 card would "fix" it straight back.

Both were kept rather than deleting the failing assertion. The pin contract and
its one exception are each worth a test, and writing only the exception would
lose what ISS-296 was for.
