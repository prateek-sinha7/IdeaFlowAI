# ISS-122

## Title

The cache-delta line is invisible in practice, because an unmetered-legacy window dilutes the percentage below the zero-suppression threshold — and the metered_runs signal that would explain this is fetched but never shown. Measured live on GET /api/analytics/summary: spend=17.744173, spend_full=17.741358, metered_runs=2. The BACKEND IS CORRECT and the FIX-239 legacy guard is PROVEN — 8 unmetered completed runs contributed exactly zero delta, so the aggregate difference (−$0.002815) equals the single metered run's delta to the cent, instead of the −$17.70 / −100% a naive sum would have produced. But as a share of a window holding $17.74 of unmetered spend, that delta is −0.016%, which rounds to 0% and hits the by-design "zero ⇒ render nothing" branch. Net effect: the line renders NOTHING even though a genuine negative delta exists, and it will stay invisible until the 30-day window turns over to metered runs. The FE already receives metered_runs and does not surface it, so a user cannot tell the difference between "no caching effect" and "not measured yet". Suggested fix (not attempted): compute the percentage over the METERED subset only (the denominator should be metered spend, not window spend), and/or always render the "Measured on N of M runs" note whenever metered_runs < completed_runs, even when the delta suppresses. NOT a defect in FIX-239's arithmetic — its guard is what prevented the catastrophic number, and that guard is verified.
