## Related

## What was wrong

Validator CRITICAL findings were emitted as events but the artifact was 
shipped anyway. The validation loop had no retry path with bounded attempts 
and no fail-closed behavior on exhaustion.

## The fix (commit 1e329e47b)

- Validation gate now re-dispatches the step on CRITICAL finding (not just event emission).
- Retry count bounded by `step.fix.max_attempts` (per-step configuration).
- On exhaustion, fail the run closed rather than shipping the artifact.
- Feedback from prior attempt included in re-dispatch context.

Pinned by test_validation_retry.py verifying retry dispatch, max_attempts 
enforcement, and closed failure on exhaustion.
