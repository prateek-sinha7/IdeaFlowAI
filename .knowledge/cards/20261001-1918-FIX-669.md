## Change

- A step whose strategy raises now fails the run visibly: an ERROR log with the step id, the step's `agent_error`, and one `pipeline_failed` with the message. The revision driver saves the reason on the run. Revision 48b0811e failed with no event, no log line and no reason.
- `json_tasks` uses a payload that is valid JSON as a whole as is; only other text gets the fence search. 48b0811e's `tasks.json` quoted fenced `json` snippets in its task bodies, and the first snippet was parsed instead. Fixture: `backend/tests/fixtures/agents/json_tasks/revision-48b0811e-tasks.json`.
