# ISS-1115

## Description

INFERRED sibling of [ISS-1094](20261007-2337-ISS-1094.md) (QA-KB-101). The live e2e tier (`tests/integration/e2e/`) and the
screens specs it implements (`tests/integration/screens/`) call the `ppt_v2` catalog card **"Pitch an idea (Detailed)"**. Its
manifest has said **"Pitch an idea (Depth)"** since `87903ab3b` (2026-10-01). The locators match a card title exactly, so every
use of the old title should find no card. Nothing was executed (see "What is INFERRED"); the strings below are CONFIRMED by reading.

## Why the root cause of QA-KB-101 implies it

ISS-1094's mechanism is a catalog label that is authored once, in a manifest, and re-typed as a literal by each test layer, with
nothing that makes a rename reach the copies. ISS-1094 is that mechanism in the backend contract test after rename 1
(`1a1b720b7`, 2026-09-25, [FIX-645](20260925-1201-FIX-645.md)). This is the same mechanism in the e2e tier after rename 2:

- `1a1b720b7` renamed `ppt_v2` to "Pitch an idea (Detailed)" AND edited the e2e locators, suites and the matching screens specs in
  the same commit, so the e2e tier was in step until then (`evidence/qa-kb-101/analysis-04-git-timeline-and-rename-commits.txt`,
  section 5).
- `87903ab3b` ("v2 names say (Depth)") renamed it to "(Depth)" and changed five manifests and no test or spec path (section 3: `0`).
- Since `1a1b720b7`, the only commit that touched those e2e and spec files is `82241ee9d` (the dev-account domain move), section 10.

## Mechanism (CONFIRMED by reading; not executed)

The label travels one deterministic path to the string the tests look for:

1. `backend/agents/workflows/ppt_v2/workflow.yaml:42`: `display_name: "Pitch an idea (Depth)"`.
2. `backend/app/api/workflows.py:398`: `display_name=(manifest.display_name if manifest else None)`, served verbatim (the in-process
   probe prints `ppt_v2 ... display_name='Pitch an idea (Depth)'`,
   `evidence/qa-kb-101/analysis-02-probe-asserted-vs-served-vs-manifest.txt`).
3. `frontend/src/components/catalog/HomeLaunchGrid.tsx:296`: `const label = row.display_name ?? getWorkflowLabel(row.id)`, rendered
   as the `<h2>` at `:332-334` and as the `Inspect ${label} details` aria-label at `:353`.
4. `tests/integration/e2e/framework/locators/home_catalog.py:35` `card(title)` = `button:has(h2:text-is("{title}"))` (exact text, by
   design, `:31-33`) and `:40` `inspect(title)` = `[aria-label="Inspect {title} details"]`.

## What is INFERRED, and what settles it

- INFERRED: that these scenarios are red today. The live tier needs a Cognito login and per-run permission (the
  [FIX-645](20260925-1201-FIX-645.md) card says the same: "Not run: local e2e needs a Cognito login"), and `test_pipeline_smoke.py`
  launches a real model run. Nothing here was started.
- Why it is a lead and not a guess: every hop above was read, and the served label was measured in process at HEAD `7adb69a3f`.
  What could still differ is the environment (a stale backend, a DB-side manifest).
- What settles it: S-02-01 alone against the running app (no model call), or `GET /api/workflows` as any user and read
  `ppt_v2.display_name`.

## Expected vs actual

Expected: every card title in the e2e lists and the specs is the title the catalog renders. Actual: one title is a rename behind.
`87903ab3b`'s message states the intent ("`Pitch an idea` and `Prototype from documents` say (Depth) instead of (Detailed)"), so the
manifest is the intended side.

## Fix direction (only once reproduced)

Test and spec side only. Change the three code strings and the six spec lines to "Pitch an idea (Depth)". Do not change the manifest
back: "(Depth)" is a recorded decision. While there, `LAUNCHABLE_TITLES` says it is "The full launchable set ... Derived from
`user_launchable: true AND is_beta: false`" (`home_catalog.py:48-50`) but names 13 titles, and the served catalog has three more such
rows: `backlog` "Write backlog from prototype and documents", `prototype_v2` "Prototype from documents (Depth)" and
`ex_playwright_smoke_test` "Browser check" (`analysis-02`). The suites only assert presence, so that gap cannot go red; it is the same
drift and worth one line in the same edit.
