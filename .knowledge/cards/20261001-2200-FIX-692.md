# FIX-692

## What was wrong

Bug report QA-KB-007. Root cause and blast radius come from [ISS-135](20260813-0005-ISS-135.md) and are not re-derived here.

`ScopedStore.append_event_at_or_after` (`backend/agents/authz.py:613`) retries a `run_events` insert whose seq
another writer took: the `except IntegrityError` branch sets `attempt_seq = tail + 1` (`:664`) and loops, and the
next `self.append_event(run_id, attempt_seq, event_id, type, payload_json)` (`:649-651`) passed the ORIGINAL
`payload_json`. The engine stamps the requested seq into that dict before persisting
(`backend/agents/execution_engine/engine.py:4192` in `execute()`, `:15917` in the resume driver) and re-stamps the
landed seq onto it only after the row committed (`:4209`, `:15928`). The row's `seq` column held the landed seq and
its stored `payload_json["seq"]` the requested one. ISS-135 counted 746 such rows on the dev DB, lag 1 to 476.

## What changed, and why there

`backend/agents/authz.py:665-669`, inside the `except IntegrityError` branch, directly after
`attempt_seq = tail + 1`:

```python
if isinstance(payload_json, dict) and "seq" in payload_json:
    payload_json = {**payload_json, "seq": attempt_seq}
```

plus a three-line comment. Every production caller routes through this one function (blast radius re-grepped at fix
time, unchanged from ISS-135): `_RunEventSink.persist` (`engine.py:3331`), fed by `execute()` and by the resume
driver, which both stamp `seq`; `_stamp_resume_marker` (`engine.py:13056`) and the durable cancel row
(`backend/app/api/run_commands.py:463`), which carry no `seq` key and are unaffected. The placement follows the four
constraints ISS-135 set:

1. Copy, never mutate. The caller's dict is the live frame. On exhaustion (`raise last_rejection`) it keeps the seq
   the caller stamped, and `persist()` returning `None` means the loops do not re-stamp it.
2. Every hop. A second collision moves the seq again; the re-stamp runs on each pass through the branch.
3. After the `if tail < attempt_seq: raise` guard (`:662-663`). A rejection the tail does not explain re-raises with
   the payload untouched.
4. Not widened. `append_event` (one dict copy per write, on every row) and dropping `seq` from stored payloads
   (REST `/events` contract, [FIX-248](20260813-0357-FIX-248.md) merge order) were left alone.

Forward only: rows already stored keep their stale payload seq. Replay merges `row.seq` over the payload
([FIX-248](20260813-0357-FIX-248.md)), and a backfill would UPDATE an append-only log.

No engine edit, no new import, no migration, no name branch. `append_event_next_seq` (`authz.py:512-590`, rewritten
by QA-KB-045 / [FIX-688](20261001-2140-FIX-688.md)) is untouched.
