# ISS-964

**INFERRED, not reproduced.** Derived while analysing QA-KB-050 ([BUG-025](20260827-BUG-025.md)). Every link below was
read in source; what has not happened is anyone driving the screen and reading the result. An unreproduced sibling is a
lead, not a defect.

Line numbers are the working tree on 2026-10-02 (HEAD `024c03ef8`). `ComposerPage.tsx`, `AgentsPopup.tsx`,
`DashboardLayout.tsx`, `page.tsx` and `SavedWorkflowsPage.tsx` carry uncommitted edits from other work, so their numbers
can be a few higher than at HEAD; `CanvasNode.tsx`, `AgentRow.tsx`, `CanvasView.tsx` and `AgentLibrary.tsx` are clean
against HEAD. The shared mechanism (the composer reads DashboardLayout's `workflowType`, which is `user_stories` on every
composer route except a built-in's canvas) is proved in [BUG-025](20260827-BUG-025.md)'s analysis section and in
`evidence/qa-kb-050/`; it is not repeated here.

## Condition

A brief of 10 or more characters typed in the Simple view of the composer on `/workflows/new` (or `/workflows/{id}/edit`).

## Why the root cause implies it (each step read)

- `ComposerPage.tsx:793-797`: `const recommendations = workflowType === "custom" ? getAgentRecommendations(ALL_LIBRARY_AGENTS, briefText, currentAgentIds).filter(...).slice(0, 6) : [];`
- `ComposerPage.tsx:798-808`: `const companionSuggestion = workflowType === "custom" ? (() => {...})() : null;`
- Rendered only in the Simple branch: "Suggested agents for your brief" at `:1515-1545`, the companion banner at `:1548-1576`.
- The route supplies `user_stories`, not `custom`, so both are `[]`/`null` on every composer route except a built-in's canvas
  ([BUG-025](20260827-BUG-025.md) mechanism steps 1-3). The feature was added for exactly this screen ([FIX-112](20260724-1217-FIX-112.md):
  "ComposerPage (the real custom-workflow entry) had no agent-recommendation logic at all") and is dead on it. Tests never see it
  because the other composer tests mount `workflowType="custom"` (`ComposerPage.manifestPersistence.test.tsx:62`,
  `ComposerPage.iss410.test.tsx:32`) and no test asserts the recommendation panel (`grep "Suggested agents for your brief"` finds
  none).

## Expected vs actual

- **Expected:** a matching brief shows "Suggested agents for your brief" chips, and a partly added companion group shows the
  amber "Add N missing" banner.
- **Actual (derived):** neither ever renders on a real composer route.

## Why this is INFERRED, and what settles it

The gate is read and the value the route supplies is executed (`evidence/qa-kb-050/analysis-proof-initialWorkflowTypeFor.out.txt`);
nobody typed a brief in the Simple view of a cold `/workflows/new`. Settle it that way, or with the unit test below.

## Where the fix belongs

`ComposerPage.tsx:793` and `:798` read the derived `canvasPipelineType` ([BUG-025](20260827-BUG-025.md) fix) instead of
`workflowType`. For a built-in's canvas the value is the built-in's type, so both stay off there, as today.

## For the test-writer

Offline: `renderComposer({ initialAgentIds: [] })` (default `workflowType` `"user_stories"`), switch to Simple, type a brief that
`getAgentRecommendations` matches against a fixture agent, and assert "Suggested agents for your brief" is shown. It fails today.
