## Why this must also be broken (derived from the ISS-378 root cause)

[ISS-378](20260828-2006-ISS-378.md)'s root cause is that each per-agent authoring surface hand-copies
its lever list, so a lever added to one surface silently misses the others. The `tools` lever has two
halves. The grants (Read/Write files) were made shared and were added to Simple view by
[FIX-350](20260828-2340-FIX-350.md); the named packs were added afterwards
([FIX-559](20260901-FIX-559.md), [ADR-0044](20260901-0324-ADR-0044.md)) to the rail only. The
ISS-274 / ISS-378 drift therefore exists a second time, for packs.

## What is missing (CONFIRMED by grep and read)

- The only pack UI is `frontend/src/components/workflow/composer/CanvasConfigRail.tsx:947-987`
  ("Tool packs", the two-entry literal at `:954-966`: `file_extract` "File extraction",
  `playwright` "Playwright browser"). `grep -rn -E "Playwright browser|File extraction|Tool packs"
  frontend/src` hits only `CanvasConfigRail.tsx:950,958,963`.
- `tool_sets` never appears in `AgentsPopup.tsx` or `composer/AgentRow.tsx`. `AdvancedExpander`
  (`AgentsPopup.tsx:1976`+, the Simple view's Configure panel) and `ConfigLeversFlat`
  (`AgentsPopup.tsx:1797-1974`, the Library and Add-agent Config tab) have no pack control.
- `hasToolOverride` (`AgentsPopup.tsx:1538-1543`) is documented as "a RESTRICTED grant (one switched
  off)" and only checks `TOOL_GRANT_LEVERS` keys, so a step whose only non-default is a pack lights
  neither the Simple-view Tools chip (`composer/AgentRow.tsx:97,216`) nor the Canvas node chip
  (`composer/CanvasNode.tsx:496`).
- The `StepSelection.tools` doc comment still says "Only `read_files`/`write_files` are
  author-editable" (`AgentsPopup.tsx:1435-1438`) although the same object carries `tool_sets`.
- ADR-0044's locked constraint places the toggles in "the Composer Tools tab" (the rail) and says
  they "round-trip through the saved manifest": the load path does put packs into the selection
  (`frontend/src/store/api/userWorkflows.ts:795-798`).
- Real workflows carry packs: `backend/agents/workflows/backlog/workflow.yaml:192-193` and
  `prototype_v2/workflow.yaml:115-116,286-287` declare `tool_sets: [file_extract]`.

## Expected vs actual

- **Expected:** if the grants are visible and editable on all three surfaces, a step's packs are too,
  or the surfaces say plainly that packs live in the rail only (the rule written into the code for
  Effort: "an Effort control on only one of them is a step whose setting depends on which view you
  opened", `AgentsPopup.tsx:2272-2275`).
- **Actual:** a document-driven step opened in Simple view shows no pack control and no indicator;
  its pack state exists only in the rail.

## Why this is INFERRED, not CONFIRMED

The absence is read, not observed in a browser. Two things are undecided and need an owner answer
before a fix is shaped: (1) whether Simple view and the Library drawer are meant to carry packs at
all (ADR-0044 says "Composer Tools tab" only; no card says packs are rail-only on purpose); (2) whether
a pack-only step should light the "Tools" chip, which today means "a grant is restricted". Neither
blocks [ISS-378](20260828-2006-ISS-378.md), whose Expected is scoped to the Read/Write grants.

To observe it: open a saved workflow or a built-in whose step declares `file_extract` in Canvas
(Tools tab shows the pack on), switch to Simple, open "Configure": no pack control, no chip.

## Where the fix belongs (if confirmed)

Export the pack list from `AgentsPopup.tsx` next to `TOOL_GRANT_LEVERS` (the rail currently inlines
its own two-entry literal at `CanvasConfigRail.tsx:954-966`, the same duplication FIX-350 removed for
grants) and render it from every surface that renders the grants. Fix
[ISS-912](20261001-2218-ISS-912.md) first or together: a pack control on a surface whose grant
checkbox clobbers `tool_sets` would drop the pack on the first grant toggle.

## Update (2026-10-02, QA-KB-016 fixer)

Still open. The precondition above, "Fix ISS-912 first", is met: [FIX-703](20261002-0051-FIX-703.md) routes every grant toggle through
`withToolGrant`, which keeps `tool_sets`. A pack control can now be added to Simple view or the drawer without the
first grant toggle dropping it. The two owner questions are still unanswered: should these surfaces carry packs,
and should a pack-only step light the Tools chip?
