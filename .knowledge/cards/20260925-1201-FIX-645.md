## Change

- Three groups in `HomeLaunchGrid`: **Start from** (product), **Engine examples**
  (collapsed; `isExampleWorkflow` on the id prefix) and **Coming Soon** (collapsed).
  Nothing stops being launchable or entitled.
- Titles: `ppt`/`ppt_v2` → "Pitch an idea (Fast)/(Detailed)", `prototype` →
  "Build an interactive prototype (Fast)", `prototype_v2` → "Prototype from
  documents (Detailed)" (shortened to fit one line). The `ex_A*` fixtures got
  plain-language titles and descriptions.
- The impeccable-redesign branch was used as a reference only: its file is 18 days
  and 288 files behind `dev`, so the ~60 lines of grouping were re-implemented.
  The hero card was not ported (needs WorkflowGraph, useLiveRunProgress and a 5s
  poll that would trip the dashboard network budget).

## Tests

rewritten from "fixtures appear as product cards" to "fixtures are launchable under
Engine examples", and its `@defect` tag dropped. Not run: local e2e needs a Cognito login.
