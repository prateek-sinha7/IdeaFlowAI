## What was wrong

Bug report QA-KB-005. There were two layers between a live `hook_run` frame and `pipelineState.hookRuns`. Both are
taken from the analysis cards, not re-derived here.

- [ISS-107](20260812-1400-ISS-107.md), the root card. `handlePipelineMessage`'s `case "hook_run"` read its payload
  only from `msg.data` and fell back to `{}` (`frontend/src/hooks/useWorkflow.ts:1703` at analysis, `:1688` just
  before this edit). The page route flattens the frame and drops the `data` key (`page.tsx`
  `handlePipelineMsgRef.current?.({ type: msg.type, ...(msg.data ...) })`). So every row took its defaults: hook
  `audit_logger`, event `""`, outcome `continue`, detail `{}`, and `created_at` from the client clock. This was the one
  case out of ten written `|| {}`. The other nine read `(msg.data as Record<string, unknown>) || msg`.
- [ISS-870](20261001-2107-ISS-870.md), the sibling. That reducer case could not be reached live. `"hook_run"` was in
  neither `pipelineTypes` (`frontend/src/app/[...view]/page.tsx:1312-1343`, which gates both dispatches) nor
  `pipelineFrameTypes` (`frontend/src/hooks/useRunStateStore.ts:531-567`, early return right after). So the payload
  loss was latent, and `hookRuns` stayed `[]` for every live run. KAN-73 ([FIX-018](20260623-FIX-018.md)) added the
  reducer case in June and never connected a routing file.

## What changed, and why there

Line numbers are as of this fix, in the uncommitted tree.

1. `frontend/src/hooks/useWorkflow.ts:1689`: `|| {}` became `|| msg`, with a one-line comment at `:1688`. The fix sits
   in the shared reducer, which both callers route through: `useWorkflow.ts:141` for page.tsx, and
   `useRunStateStore.ts:587` for the store. Patching the page dispatch would have left the reducer out of step with its
   nine siblings. The store's `flatMsg` (`useRunStateStore.ts:575-577`) spreads `...msg`, so `data` survives and that
   path still takes the `msg.data` branch. The optional "strip `type` from `detail`" was skipped, as the card advised,
   because nothing reads either key.
2. `frontend/src/app/[...view]/page.tsx:1341-1343`: `"hook_run"` was added to `pipelineTypes` with a two-line
   comment. Both cards required the reducer fix to land first. Opening the gate before it would have filled `hookRuns`
   with default rows.
3. `frontend/src/hooks/useRunStateStore.ts:567-569`: `"hook_run"` was added to `pipelineFrameTypes` the same way.
4. `AGENT_SCOPED_FRAME_TYPES` (`frontend/src/lib/wsReplayState.ts:136-151`) was left unchanged. ISS-870 left this
   decision to the fixer. The set's own contract (`wsReplayState.ts:120-135`) covers frames keyed by `agent_id` that
   change per-agent live state. `hook_run` appends a run-level row. Both dispatches already sit behind the run-id
   guards at `page.tsx:1528-1561` (`isForeignFrame`, `isForActiveRun`), the same guards the lifecycle frames rely on.
   Replays stay deduplicated, because `hook_run` is already in the ISS-082 `ACCUMULATING_FRAME_TYPES` redelivery gate
   (`useWorkflow.ts:275`), and the store's `flatMsg` keeps the outer `seq` and `event_id`.

What the user sees: nothing yet. Live audit rows now carry their real `event`, their `detail`, and the server
`timestamp`. But `RunChatLane.deriveSecurityBullets` (`RunChatLane.tsx:469`) keeps only rows with
`outcome === "block"` and a `secret|scan` hook, and no live source emits one. That gap is
[ISS-871](20261001-2107-ISS-871.md). `AuditTab` receives `hookRuns` as a prop (`PreviewPanel.tsx:1974`) but never
reads it.

Blast radius, re-checked by grep before editing (non-test `frontend/src`): one writer of `hookRuns` (the reducer), two
readers (`RunChatLane.tsx:469` and the `PreviewPanel.tsx:1974` prop), and two reducer callers (`useWorkflow.ts:141`
and the store's `handleFrame`). This matches the ISS-107 card.

## Not fixed: ISS-871 (left open)

[ISS-871](20261001-2107-ISS-871.md): no backend path emits a `secret_scan` block as a `hook_run` frame, and none of
the four `emit_hook_event` callers sends a `hook` key. The options are adding an emitter, feeding the bullet from
persisted rows, or deleting the bullet. That choice is a design decision, so this fix does not touch it, and the card
stays `open`. Its option 1 needed "ISS-870 and the QA-KB-005 reducer fix" to land first, and both now have.

## Not fixed: pre-existing red in terminalReopenReconcile.source.test.ts (ISS-898, left open)
