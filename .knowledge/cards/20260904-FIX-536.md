## Related

## What was wrong

pdf_to_prototype writes a versioned draft chain (draft.v1 → draft.v2 → ...) 
because ~50 parallel builders write concurrently and write_file refuses to 
overwrite. The final deliverable name must stay static (prototype.html), so 
there must be a deterministic copy from whichever draft is the latest to the 
static name. The ONLY thing that ever produced a correct prototype.html was 
the existing deterministic byte-for-byte copy in SingleFileResolver.resolve() 
at end-of-run deliverable resolution. Asking a model to reproduce that copy, 
or to narrate into it, truncated the 60-90 KB document.

## The fix (commit 1e329e47b)

- New step kind `mechanical: "publish"`, defined in `backend/agents/capabilities/publish_step.py`.
- Engine hook short-circuits BEFORE strategy resolution (not registered as a capability).
- Reuses `SingleFileResolver._highest_glob_match()`, never re-implementing the glob logic.
- Raised during pipeline construction (plan.py:Step.mechanical) and inserted as its own step.
- Deterministic, no model call, no tokens spent: pure disk copy from draft chain to deliverable.

Pinned by test_mechanical_publish_step.py verifying the glob match and byte copy.
