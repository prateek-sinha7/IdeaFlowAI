## Related

## What was wrong

Two defects surfaced the moment parallel readers really overlapped (FIX-574):
`extract_text` ran synchronously on the asyncio loop, so one large PDF froze
every request in the server, and two readers entering pdfium at once crashed
the process with SIGBUS inside `FPDF_*`.

## The fix (commit 127b20a3d)

Both tools are `async def` and push the pdfium work to a thread; a module-level
`_PDFIUM_LOCK` keeps those threads out of pdfium concurrently. Readers still
overlap on everything else (model calls, text handling); only the pdfium
critical section is serial.

## Trade-off

Page rendering across five readers is serialised. That is the correct ceiling
until pdfium is isolated per process; do not "optimise" the lock away.
