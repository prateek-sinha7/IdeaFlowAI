## Related

## What was wrong

The `is_leaf` calculation in the compiled plan identified which steps can be
terminal by checking if any jump targets (from conditional gates' outcomes and
default_next) pointed to them. A gate routing to an earlier step (a loop-back,
e.g., Coverage Check → Synthesizer on "incomplete") added that earlier step to
`jump_target_idx`. This marked the STEP BEFORE the gate as a false leaf (its
normal array-adjacent successor became a jump target). Runs would therefore
complete immediately after the parallel reading group, never reaching Synthesizer.

## The fix (commit 127b20a3d)

- `jump_target_idx` now only accepts FORWARD targets: `if index_of[outcome.target] > src_idx`.
- Loop-back targets (lower index) are skipped, as they are legitimate conditional
  routing but do not identify leaf nodes.
- This distinction is applied to both `step.route.outcomes` and `step.route.default_next`.
