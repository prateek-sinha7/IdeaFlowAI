## Change

- New `backlog` workflow under `backend/agents/workflows/backlog/`, with two
  mechanical steps (`assemble_backlog`, `extract_prototype_ui`) wired through the
  compiler allow-list, engine dispatch and registry discovery.
- Capabilities `backlog_prep`, `backlog_assemble`, `backlog_export`. Export renders
  the backlog to PDF and ZIP using vendored Manrope fonts and Mermaid
  (`agents/capabilities/vendor/`); `markdown-it-py` added.
- `.html`/`.htm` uploads are accepted by the upload and extract endpoints and
  materialised screen by screen by `app/agents/tools/html_extract.py` (headless
  browser, static fallback).
- `backlog` entitled on every tier, backend and frontend.

Design and tasks: `specs/021-backlog/`, `BACKLOG_WORKFLOW.md`.
