## What is broken

[FIX-646](20261002-0332-FIX-646.md) restored the disk-skill block in
`agents/factory.py::_compose_system_prompt`, which renders `ctx.disk_skill` into the
`skills` slot. Only the `default` order (`DEFAULT_ORDER`, `policy.py:67`) has that slot:

- `BODY_ONLY_ORDER = ("prompt_body",)` (`policy.py:81`)
- `GOVERNANCE_ORDER = ("guardrails", "hooks", "constitution", "prompt_body")` (`policy.py:85`)

When this was measured at `75a3c207`, 55 agents used `body_only`, 39 used `governance`
and 1 (`file-analyst`) used the default. A user who saves a per-agent skill through
`POST /api/agents/skills` for any of the other 94 agents sees it accepted and never
reaching the agent.

## Disposition

Open. The fix is a product decision (Requirement 29), not a bug fix. Options are to add
`skills` to `GOVERNANCE_ORDER`, which changes the prompts of 39 agents and their
goldens, or to stop offering per-agent skills for agents whose policy ignores them.
