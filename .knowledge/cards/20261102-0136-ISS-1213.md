## What is broken

Anthropic's vision documentation (platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/vision,
fetched 2026-11-02) says a request with more than 20 images is held to a stricter
per-image dimension limit. It advises either resizing every image to at most 3,000 px on
its longest side, or keeping the request to 20 images or fewer. Images over the stricter
limit are rejected with an `invalid_request_error`.

The guard does not enforce this. `_ImagePayloadGuardMiddleware._prune` in
`backend/app/agents/deep_agent_runner.py` checks byte size and the 8000 px side limit,
and it looks only at `ToolMessage` blocks. Unseen images from the current turn are never
evicted (FIX-550), so the number of images in one request is not bounded. An image
between 3,000 and 8,000 px per side therefore reaches the provider whole, and a turn with
more than 20 of them fails.

Two paths bypass the guard completely. `agents/capabilities/input_providers/pdf_pages.py`
and `run_images.py` put image blocks into the prompt rather than into tool results. The
pdf_pages pixel ceiling is `_IMAGE_MAX_PIXELS = 5120 * 5120`, which is above 3,000 px per side.

## Evidence

Not reproduced. No run has shown this error. The finding comes from the documentation and
from reading the guard's `_prune` and the two input providers.

## Disposition

Open. Next steps:
1. Reproduce: send 21 PNGs of 3,500 px per side in one request through the guard.
2. Decide between a request-level cap in `_prune` and a resize at injection in
   `pdf_pages.py` and `run_images.py`.

The tiles from FIX-786 are 2,000 px per side, so they satisfy both the 2,000 px and the
3,000 px figures.
