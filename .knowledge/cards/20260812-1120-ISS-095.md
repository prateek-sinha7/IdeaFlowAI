# ISS-095

## Title

**3 reds in `tests/agents/test_declared_gate_streaming.py`, and the register's "environmental" label for them looks WRONG — a real defect may be hiding behind it.** `.planning/IMPLEMENTATION-REGISTER.md:4075` records these as *"`declared_gate_streaming` 3 env-reds (sqlite FK, Postgres-gated)"*. **The observed failures are not sqlite FK errors.** Measured offline at `fab9b646` and again after FIX-231: **3 failed**, unchanged, in ~17 s. `:203` `AssertionError: no review_gate_ready was ever received`; `:264` `AssertionError: precondition: the static inline gate set changed: {'prototype-analyze', 'prototype-specify', 'prototype-plan'}`; `:474` `AssertionError: rejection never produced pipeline_cancelled; got [... 'pipeline_complete']`. These are assertions that a **declared human gate never fired** and that a **gate rejection did not cancel the run** — product-shaped claims, not environment noise. (sqlite FK warnings DO appear in this suite's stderr, but they are the engine's documented best-effort `REQ-PERSIST-02`/`REQ-PERSIST-03` degrade path and are logged as warnings on passing tests too, so they are not what fails these three.) The third symptom is plausibly the same defect as **ISS-091** (gate rejection does not stop the pipeline); the first two may be a declared-gate arming regression. **NOT root-caused here — deliberately.** This row exists because a mislabelled "environmental" red is how a real defect stays invisible, which is exactly the failure mode that let ISS-074 sit for 43 days. **First action for whoever takes this: correct or confirm the IMPLEMENTATION-REGISTER:4075 label, then re-baseline at a pre-regression commit to date it.** Note this file deliberately does NOT monkeypatch `_run_review_gate` (see its docstring at line 51), so it is not ISS-074 and was not fixed by FIX-231.
