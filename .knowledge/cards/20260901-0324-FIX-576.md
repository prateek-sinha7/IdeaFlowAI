## Related

## What was wrong

A composed (Composer-built) run's pipeline existed only in the launch request.
On resume after a crash the engine rebuilt the roster from the `custom` registry
entry, which knows nothing about the composition, and ran a different pipeline
under the same run id.

## The fix (commit 127b20a3d)

Migration `0040_run_manifest_json` adds `workflow_runs.manifest_json`. Launch
stores the compiled manifest there; resume recompiles from it and refuses to
continue when compilation fails, rather than guessing a roster.

## How to verify

Kill a composed run mid-pipeline and resume it: the roster banner names the
composer's steps, not the generic custom agents.
