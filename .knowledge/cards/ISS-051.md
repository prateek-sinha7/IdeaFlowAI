# ISS-051

## Title

A nested revision reaches depth 2 with BOTH levels computing revision_index=1, so two passes collide on the same :rev1 checkpoint thread (measured: 7 dispatches, only 4 unique thread ids). The inner finally (engine.py:5325) also resets ectx.revision_attempt to 0 AND wipes ectx.spec_revision_context while the outer pass is still on the stack. Reachable TODAY, independent of ISS-050: the analyze re-run inside the sub-pipeline opens its own gate with update_specs_eligible=True.

## Source / evidence

Observed by the 260811-mxg verifier via instrumentation; reproduced offline by the si4 planner. Duplicate ids + counter reset confirmed; a resulting bad spec NOT observed.
