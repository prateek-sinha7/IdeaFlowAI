## What was wrong

`ChallengeForm` in `frontend/src/app/login/page.tsx` displayed a validation error
set by `handleChallengeSubmit` (e.g. "Passwords do not match.") but none of the four
field `onChange` handlers (`newPassword`, `confirmNewPassword`, `selectedFactor`,
`mfaCode`) ever cleared it — same mechanism as ISS-244 in `AccountSettings.tsx`.
The banner would persist after the user corrected the mismatch until the next submit.

## What changed

`frontend/src/app/login/page.tsx` only:

- Added `onClearError: () => void` to `ChallengeForm`'s props interface with a
  clarifying doc-comment.
- All four `onChange` handlers now call `onClearError()` alongside their own setter.
- At the `ChallengeForm` call site in `LoginForm`, passed `onClearError={() => setError("")}`.

`setError` itself remains exclusively in `LoginForm`'s closure — the presentational
`ChallengeForm` never reaches across its boundary to the parent's state directly.
