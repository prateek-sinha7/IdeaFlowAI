## Related

## What was wrong

Redoable behavior was a hardcoded literal at all gate sites (ISS-090), 
applied uniformly to all gates. There was no per-gate or per-step configuration 
to enable or disable redo for specific gates.

## The fix (commit 1e329e47b)

- Human gates can declare `redoable: true` in their configuration.
- Redo is bounded by `step.redo_attempts` (per-step limit).
- On exhaustion, the gate closes without allowing further redos (fail-closed).
- Gates that do not declare `redoable: true` cannot be redone.

Pinned by test_declared_gate_redo.py verifying that only declared redoable 
gates can be redone, and redos are bounded by step-level configuration.
