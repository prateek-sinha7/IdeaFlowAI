## Related

## What was wrong

Wave workers were dispatched without unique per-worker identity stamps, 
making it difficult to distinguish individual workers in logs and events. 
Parent-level lifecycle events were not emitted, and sandbox round-trip 
validation was not performed before target-missing errors.

## The fix (commit 1e329e47b)

- Each worker is stamped with a unique `worker_id` derived from the wave and task.
- Lifecycle events emitted: `wave_started`, `wave_completed`, `wave_failed`.
- Before reporting target-missing error, sandbox round-trip is tested 
  (prevents false target-missing if sandbox is dead).
- Parent run receives visibility into wave-level progress and failures.

Pinned by test_wave_scheduler.py verifying per-worker identity stamps, 
lifecycle event emissions, and sandbox round-trip validation.
