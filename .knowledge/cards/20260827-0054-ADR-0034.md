## Decision

Spec 017 T3 said to wrap `verify_layout.py` unmodified. It was modified, and this
records why — the deviation is the decision.

## The measurement

With the bundled folder present, all 18 Office-safe families measure. Without it,
on this macOS box, 9 do:

```
WITH     measurable 18/18   UNVERIFIED: none
WITHOUT  measurable  9/18   UNVERIFIED: Calibri, Cambria, Candara, Consolas,
                                        Constantia, Corbel, Garamond,
                                        Palatino Linotype, Segoe UI
```

Calibri is the one that matters most: `html-deck-to-pptx` maps most sans families
(DM Sans, Inter, Poppins, Montserrat) onto **Arial or Calibri**, and macOS ships
Arial but not Calibri. A bare Linux container has the mirror problem — Calibri
absent AND Arial absent.

## Why UNVERIFIED is the danger

The verifier exists to catch text that does not fit its box and lines that print
through each other. A shape it cannot measure is not flagged — it is skipped. So a
host-fonts-only verifier does not fail loudly on an unmeasurable deck; it returns
`ok` on a deck it never checked. The script states it plainly: *"a check that
cannot measure most of the deck is a check that passes broken decks."*

## Cost

~11 MB of TTFs in the repo. Fixed, never changes, and the alternative is a layout
gate whose verdict depends on which machine ran it.
