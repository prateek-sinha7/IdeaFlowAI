## Change

The assembler drew each dependency, build-order and screen-map chart as one graph, and the PDF shrank the largest to unreadable scales (0.42). Each chart now splits into parts of at most 15 boxes and 20 arrows, in a shape that fits the page: LR at most 2 columns by 8 rows, TD at most 2 wide by 7 deep. Edges to another part end at a dashed stub box. Back edges are dropped when ranking. The lowest print scale on the 78e185fb backlog is now 0.78.

Per spec 022 section 11.7 (user decision 16:02, 2026-10-01).
