## Related

## What was wrong

Run `b0642e0f` (72m, one agent failed) was persisted `degraded` with a
`completed_at`, and its history row still said "Running" eighteen hours later.
`StatusBadge` resolved its pill through four `if`s and an else-fallthrough, so
any status not explicitly handled — `degraded` among them — silently inherited
the in-flight label.

## The fix (commit 3e6fcb93c)

The pill table is keyed on `Exclude<WorkflowStatus, InFlightStatus>`, making it
total: adding a terminal status without labelling it is a type error rather than
a wrong badge shipped to production.
