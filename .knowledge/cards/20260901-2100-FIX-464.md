## What was wrong

`frontend/src/app/workflow/create/page.tsx`'s `CreateRoute()` silently defaulted any
unrecognized `raw` mode to `"prototype"` with no `router.replace`, no error state.
If `proxy.ts`'s redirect did not run (cached response, CDN bypass, matcher miss), the
URL and rendered content diverged.

## What changed

`frontend/src/app/workflow/create/page.tsx`:

- Added `useRouter` and `useEffect` imports.
- `isKnownMode` checks `raw === "prototype" || raw === "ppt" || raw === "ppt_v2"`.
- When `raw` is non-null and not a known mode: `router.replace('...?mode=prototype')`
  is called in a `useEffect`, and the component returns `null` while the redirect is
  in flight — same null-while-redirecting pattern as the auth gate in `page.tsx`.
- A `null` raw (no `?mode=` at all) passes through to the prototype default unchanged —
  this is the `proxy.ts`-handled normal path; no regression.
