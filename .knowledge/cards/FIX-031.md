## Description

ISS-029 (KAN-63 regression): od_ppt example.html leaked into the deck PLANNER (od-ppt-brief-analyst), not just the builder — degraded planning and left the od_ppt characterization golden red for ~3 weeks

## Root cause

KAN-63 (b5f5885e, Jun 15) widened the opendesign example.html gate from is_builder to (is_builder or "workspace" in spec_tools); the "workspace" proxy was too broad and swept in the planner-shaped od-ppt-brief-analyst (which declares tools:[workspace]), feeding a full worked HTML example to a planner (violates the Phase-7 "planners must not see example.html" rule), and the od_ppt event golden (which pins context_message) was never regenerated so INV-3 diverged. Fix (B-explicit): re-key the gate on a DECLARED inject — (is_builder or "template_example" in injects) — and add template_example to od-ppt-composer's injects so the composer opts in explicitly while the brief-analyst stays example-free; regenerated ONLY the od_ppt golden (composer gains the block, brief-analyst unchanged, other 4 goldens byte-identical).
