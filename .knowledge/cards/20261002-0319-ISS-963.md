# ISS-963

**INFERRED, not reproduced.** Derived while analysing QA-KB-050 ([BUG-025](20260827-BUG-025.md)). Every link below was
read in source; what has not happened is anyone driving the screen and reading the result. An unreproduced sibling is a

Line numbers are the working tree on 2026-10-02 (HEAD `024c03ef8`). `ComposerPage.tsx`, `AgentsPopup.tsx`,
`DashboardLayout.tsx`, `page.tsx` and `SavedWorkflowsPage.tsx` carry uncommitted edits from other work, so their numbers
can be a few higher than at HEAD; `CanvasNode.tsx`, `AgentRow.tsx`, `CanvasView.tsx` and `AgentLibrary.tsx` are clean

composer route except a built-in's canvas) is proved in [BUG-025](20260827-BUG-025.md)'s analysis section and in
`evidence/qa-kb-050/`; it is not repeated here.

## Condition

Any `CanvasNode` or `AgentRow` that renders an agent whose `getRole` is `"required"` **in the canvas's own pipeline**, so the
type is correct and the [BUG-025](20260827-BUG-025.md) type fix does not touch it:

- the built-in canvases `/workflows/{type}/canvas` for `user_stories` (4 of 6 nodes are required), `app_builder` (13 of 15),
  `mulesoft_to_springboot` (11 of 13) and `dotnet_to_azure` (11 of 13); the node lists are the `agent:` lines of
  `backend/agents/workflows/<type>/workflow.yaml`, the roles are `AgentsPopup.tsx:169-203`. `ppt` and `prototype` are all
  locked, so the badge and the lock agree there, which is what `S-04-03` (Core x3 on `/workflows/ppt/canvas`) asserts;
- the launch modal's embedded canvas (`AgentsPopup.tsx:2983-2986`, which passes its real `pipelineType`).

## Why the root cause implies it (each step read)

BUG-025's compounding defect 1 (see its analysis section) is that the two renderers compute two predicates from the one role:

- `frontend/src/components/workflow/composer/CanvasNode.tsx:479-480`: `const locked = role === "locked"; const removable = role === "optional";`
  Badge `{locked && (...Core...)}` at `:731`; Remove `disabled={!removable}` at `:639`, titled `"Core agents can't be removed"`, styled
  `disabled:opacity-0` at `:644`.
- `frontend/src/components/workflow/composer/AgentRow.tsx:85-86` is the same pair; badge at `:170`, Remove at `:199-201`
  (greyed with `disabled:text-line-faint`, so visible but unexplained).

`"required"` is neither locked nor optional, so a required agent gets no badge and a disabled Remove. On the Canvas the dead
control is also invisible. The export comment at `AgentsPopup.tsx:207-209` says the helper serves "the per-agent Core/Required
lock badge", i.e. the badge was meant to cover both roles; the modal's own guard is `getRole(agentId, pipelineType) !== "optional"`
(`AgentsPopup.tsx:2823`), so "required means not removable" is the designed rule and the badge is what is missing. The modal's
legend says only "Core = locked" (`:2970`).

Executed (`evidence/qa-kb-050/analysis-proof-getRole.out.txt`): `user_stories`, `epic-architect` gives role `required`, Core badge
false, Remove enabled false.

## Expected vs actual

- **Expected:** the lock and the badge agree, and a locked control is visible (BUG-025 "Expected").
- **Actual (derived):** on those canvases the required nodes show no Core badge and, on the Canvas, an invisible Remove that
  does nothing and carries the title "Core agents can't be removed".

## Why this matters for QA-KB-050

After the type fix `/workflows/new` is all-optional, so a verifier that only checks `/workflows/new` would pass the bug
while this half of BUG-025's "badge and lock disagree" stays open on the built-in canvases and the modal. It needs its own
check.

## Why this is INFERRED, and what settles it

Predicates and styling are read and the role table is executed; nobody has opened `/workflows/user_stories/canvas` and read
the DOM. Settle it there: `[aria-label="Remove Backlog Architecture Agent"]` should be `disabled` with no `Core` text in its node.

## Where the fix belongs

One predicate, defined once beside `getRole` (`AgentsPopup.tsx:210`) and called by `CanvasNode.tsx:479-480`,
`AgentRow.tsx:85-86` and `AgentsPopup.handleRemove` (`:2823`): Core badge and Remove lock both read `role !== "optional"`; the
drag guard stays `role !== "locked"` so required steps remain reorderable. Option (a), badge every non-optional role, matches the
designed rule above; option (b), making `required` removable on canvases, changes the modal guard and the built-in contract and
is a product call, not part of this card. Separately, `CanvasNode.tsx:644` should use `AgentRow.tsx:201`'s greyed state instead of
`disabled:opacity-0`. The guard itself stays: BUG-025's constraint 2 and `S-04-03` (`/workflows/ppt/canvas` Core x3).

## For the test-writer

Offline, `CanvasNode.kindIcon.test.tsx:8-12` is a ready harness: render `<CanvasNode {...base} agent={epic-architect}
pipelineType={"user_stories"} />` and assert the Remove button is disabled **iff** a `Core` badge is present. It fails today
(disabled, no badge). Add the same for `AgentRow`, and a class-string check that the disabled Remove does not carry
`disabled:opacity-0` (jsdom does not compute styles, so assert on `className` as `AgentRow.test.tsx` does for the chips).
