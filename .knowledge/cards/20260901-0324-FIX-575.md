## Related

## What was wrong

`engine.py` derived `is_leaf` from every jump target in the plan. A conditional
gate that routes BACK to an earlier step (a loop-back) therefore made the step
just before its target a leaf. Observed live: a 12-step composed run stopped
right after its parallel reading group and reported success.

## The fix (commit 127b20a3d)

`is_leaf` now counts only forward jump targets. Backward and self jumps are
treated as loops (engine.py around the `# A backward (or self) "step" jump is a
LOOP` comment) and never terminate the run.
