## Related

## What was wrong

Prototype screens sometimes omitted required form fields that were mentioned 
in the specification. There was no deterministic post-build validation to 
ensure all specified fields were implemented before returning the prototype.

## The fix (commit 1e329e47b)

- New deterministic validator: `fragment_field_coverage`.
- Extracts required form field names from journeys specification.
- Checks prototype HTML for presence of each required field.
- CRITICAL finding if any required field is missing or misnamed.
- No model call: pure logic over spec and HTML.

Pinned by test_validator_fragment_field_coverage.py with fixtures showing 
good and bad form field implementations.
