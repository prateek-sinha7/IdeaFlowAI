# ADR-0008

## Decision

In the context of ADR-0003 making the compiled plan the run's roster, facing a composer
selection of 3 agents silently executing all 9 steps the static `custom` manifest
declares, we decided the roster has three ordered sources and the plan FILLS IN rather
than OVERRULES — the caller's list wins whenever it has one — to achieve a runnable
composed workflow that does not eat a user's agent picks, accepting that "the plan is
the single source" is now "the plan is the source of last resort".

**Area:** engine, api, workflow
