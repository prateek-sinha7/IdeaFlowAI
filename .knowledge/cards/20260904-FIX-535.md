## What was wrong

Opening a saved workflow in the Composer and pressing Save rewrote
`manifest_json` through `agentsToManifestSteps`, which emitted only the keys the
canvas models. On the "PDF to Prototype v2" row that collapsed three
`wave_scheduler` steps to `single_shot` — losing `fanout`, `task_source` and
`injects` — wiped `depends_on` on five steps, and dropped every `consumes` /
`produces` / `validators` / `fix` / `require_render` contract. The result was a
row that could no longer run, produced by a save that changed nothing the user
could see.

## The fix (commit 00b61ff93)

`manifestStepToAgent` captures every step key outside `MODELED_STEP_KEYS` into
`AgentDef.extra`; `agentToManifestStep` re-spreads it first so canvas-owned
fields still win on conflict. Two defaults that used to fire unconditionally are
now conditional (leaf strategy, `spawn_subagents`), because both were clobbering
author intent straight back out.

Covered by a fixture of the real manifest plus seven round-trip tests; five fail
without the change.
