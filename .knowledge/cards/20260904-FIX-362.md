## Related

## What was wrong

Upload types were defined in multiple places (frontend and backend), causing 
drift and inconsistencies. Manifest-to-canvas conversion was scattered across 
modules, losing step-level and manifest-level keys on save/load cycles.

The Composer round trip (open → modify → save → open again) was dropping 
step-level keys (strategy, fanout, validators, depends_on, input_providers) 
and manifest-level keys (id, display_name, limits, input_providers, seed_files). 
Upload types lacked a single source of truth, leading to inconsistencies 
between client and server definitions.

## The fix (commit 1e329e47b)

Landed in commit `1e329e47b` with the files listed under `globs`; the tests under `verification.test_files` pin the behaviour.
