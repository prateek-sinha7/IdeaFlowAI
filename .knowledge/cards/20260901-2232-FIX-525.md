## Related

## What was wrong

`:redo{n}`, `:rev{n}` and `:retry{n}` each mint a fresh checkpoint thread. A
conditional-route loop-back had no such suffix, so the re-entered step resumed
its earlier conversation and answered from memory. Observed live: a Coverage
Check returned its previous "incomplete" verdict in seven seconds without
re-reading the file the Synthesizer had already corrected, so the loop could
never converge and exhausted its budget.

## The fix (commit 97717eb9f)

The engine counts dispatches per step and derives the thread id from that
count. Counting from `step_visit_counts` was considered and rejected: it is
incremented only for the route target, which left the gate step itself on its
old thread.
