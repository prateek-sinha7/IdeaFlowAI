## Related

**Related to:** ADR-0044 (tool-pack contract for composed workflows).

## What was wrong

ToolPermissions had no concept of which tool_sets were safe to allow in
user-authored (untrusted) composed workflows. The Composer's tool-pack toggles
needed an explicit allow-list to prevent enabling sets that access external systems
or require template-level authority.

## The fix (commit 127b20a3d)

- Add `tool_sets` list to ToolPermissions (trusted cap includes all nine sets).
- The untrusted cap lists only `["file_extract", "playwright"]` — the two that
  operate within the run sandbox and do not touch external systems.
- The Composer's per-step tool-pack toggles are gated on this allow-list; users
  cannot enable sets outside of it.
- Documented: playwright is `user_allowed=False` on the palette but deliberately
  grantable per-step here, as it drives a browser against the run's own artifacts.
