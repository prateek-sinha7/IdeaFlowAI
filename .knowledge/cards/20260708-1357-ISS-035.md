# ISS-035

## Title

Run chat lane shows no "Cancelled by you" acknowledgement after Stop — `pipeline_cancelled` sets no cancelled flag and pushes no chat message, so `runLaneState` falls through to `idle` (violates LIVE-STATE-CONTRACT §1). New-lane manifestation of the ISS-007 class; the revived RunChatLane never got the equivalent cancel ack. **CLOSED in Phase 32 plan 32-05** (code landed but the register row stayed stale — the tracking gap noted in `IMPLEMENTATION-REGISTER.md` §Phase-32): the `pipeline_cancelled` reducer now stamps a derivable `cancelled: true` terminal marker (symmetric with `pipeline_failed`'s `failed: true`), and `RunChatLane.tsx` (32-06) renders the "Cancelled by you" + relaunch card off that marker (`data-testid="chat-terminal-cancelled"`); the FIX-039 unconditional accumulator reset stayed byte-unchanged. VERIFIED in `32-VERIFICATION.md` Truth #8 (VERIFIED against the codebase, not the SUMMARY).
