# ADR-0013

## Decision

Cognito is the sole interactive credential authority. The backend verifies only configured-pool
RS256 Cognito access tokens and resolves their `sub` into a local `User` ownership/mapping row.
The application does not mint or accept a local JWT/password/break-glass credential fallback.

Effective tier and administrator authority derive from the verified `cognito:groups` claim.
`User.tier` and `User.is_admin` remain refreshable local projections for listing, operational
state, and persistence joins; they must not overrule the current token. A missing local mapping
may be JIT-provisioned only after provider authentication succeeds and only under the documented
fail-closed collision checks.

## Context

The committed Phase 1–9 cutover removes `password_hash` and `auth_provider`, makes
`cognito_sub` non-null after a data preflight, adds provider-aware token validity and lifecycle
controls, and moves invitation, group, disable, deletion, and retry semantics behind Cognito
operations. Terraform/SSM/deployment configuration and the pre-migration link gate are part of
the same operating model.

## Consequences

- Route, SSE, handoff, API-key, and admin dependencies must converge on the shared principal
  model rather than reimplement credential parsing or reading the local projection as authority.
- Role/tier changes must change provider groups, invalidate prior sessions, then refresh the
  local projection.
- Deleting a local user does not make provider cleanup optional: failed final deletion is recorded
  durably for retry after the account has already been disabled and local work products removed.
- Any future provider migration must be a deliberate replacement decision with an explicit
  cutover/data-preflight plan; adding a local fallback would violate this ADR.
