# ISS-028

## Title

**app_builder task-loop partial failure can report a clean `pipeline_complete` (no `degraded`).** Surfaced by the 2026-06-14 gap-audit of ISS-016. `engine.py:2141-2143` computes `_unrecovered_failed = _failed_agent_ids - {r.agent_id for r in results}`. In the prototype/build task-loop, ONE agent_id (e.g. `prototype-build`) runs across all tasks; if task K succeeds (appends a `prototype-build` result) and task K+1 hard-errors (adds `prototype-build` to `_failed_agent_ids`, returns without appending), the subtraction zeroes it → the run emits `pipeline_complete` with NO `status:degraded` over a half-built deliverable. **Pre-existing** (the agent_id-keyed subtraction is the Phase-13 WR-05 fix, NOT introduced by ISS-016/16-01), LOW-probability (a hard `ValidationException` is account/entitlement-level → all-or-nothing per ISS-018; a realistic mid-build transient is a throttle, correctly retried), and OUTSIDE ISS-016's stated all-fail/agent-granular-partial scope (both of which work + are tested). Belongs to the already-DEFERRED ISS-016 "second signal" (empty-but-clean→degraded) follow-up. **Proper fix (future):** key the degraded decision on `(agent_id, task_number)` rather than collapsing on agent_id. Do NOT reopen ISS-016.
