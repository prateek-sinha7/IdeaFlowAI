# ISS-151

## Title

A test's negative assertion has gone stale and can no longer pass, for reasons unrelated to what it guards. backend/tests/agents/test_concierge_capability.py::test_compose_system_prompt_injects_chain_hints_block fails at its final line, assert "chained into" not in no_hints and "follow-up" not in no_hints (line 261) — this asserts the BASE Concierge system prompt (no chain_hints supplied) contains neither literal, so the chain-hints block's own presence can be detected by contrast. Both literals are now present in the base prompt unconditionally and for reasons that have nothing to do with chain hints: "Never ask the user follow-up questions before calling a propose_* tool" (RESPONSE RULES) and "chain it into a follow-up workflow" / "chained into" (INTENT ROUTING). Confirmed pre-existing and unrelated to FIX-249: independently reproduced BOTH before and after that fix, byte-identical failure both times, and FIX-249's own new honesty-rule sentence was confirmed to contain neither literal. The test's POSITIVE half (no_hints == empty_hints) still passes and is unaffected. Needs a decision: rewrite the negative assertion against a literal unique to the actual chain-hints block (e.g. "Available follow-up workflows"), or drop the negative half of the guard as no longer a meaningful contrast.
