# ISS-087

## Title

The un-versioned halves of the agent-detail cards do not follow the artifact version picker. FIX-226 made every CONTENT-derived surface follow the selection, but three inputs to deriveArtifactCardModel are run/agent state rather than artifact content and were deliberately left pinned to the agent: protoCompletedTasks (the "Task plan · N planned" rows), validationPassed/validationIssues (the checks pass/fail badge) and dagEdges (the handoff line). Selecting an older <tasks> version therefore shows that version's card TYPE with the run's CURRENT task rows. Making these follow the selection is not a re-thread — the FE has no per-version copy of any of them, so it needs either a parse of the selected <tasks> body (which would change the LATEST-version behaviour the 42-09 tests pin) or the registered backend follow-ups F1/F2 (AgentDetailPanel.tsx:597-607 — a {coverage,counts{P0..P3}} aggregate on GET /runs/{id}/validation-results, and an event-free sections extractor). Low impact: the task-planner agent produces one version on most runs, and the mismatch is invisible unless a run revised its task list.

## Source / evidence

Identified while fixing ISS-085 (quick-260812-77g) and recorded rather than silently fixed or silently ignored; the boundary is stated in AgentDetailPanel.tsx:745-751 and in the FIX-226 register row. Not observed by a user.
