## What was wrong

Bug report QA-KB-046. `backend/tests/unit/agents/test_revision_gating.py` ran
`1 failed, 6 passed`: `test_declared_revision_with_parent_seeds_after_assert_owns`
failed as `[XPASS(strict)] ISS-633 unfixed`. The test body passed. Only its
marker was wrong.

## Root cause

[ISS-642](20260831-1842-ISS-642.md) has the full diagnosis (analysis, 2026-10-01).
It is not re-derived here.

- `835793a99` decorated the test with
  `@pytest.mark.xfail(reason="ISS-633 unfixed", strict=True)` (`test_revision_gating.py:186`
  at HEAD) while it still expected the read list from before the parent-uploads seed.
- `1e329e47b` repaired the harness. Both expected lists now end with
  `.uploads/manifest.json` (`test_revision_gating.py:230-245`). That commit left the
  marker in place.
- The production defect behind ISS-633 was fixed separately by
  [FIX-432](20260831-1842-FIX-432.md).
- With `strict=True`, a passing test fails the run.

## Change

One line deleted in `backend/tests/unit/agents/test_revision_gating.py`: the
`xfail` decorator on `test_declared_revision_with_parent_seeds_after_assert_owns`.

- `@pytest.mark.issue("ISS-633")` stays as the pointer. `backend/pyproject.toml:174`
  sets that convention: "issue: the ISS card this test proves; paired with xfail
  until the fix lands".
- `@pytest.mark.issue("ISS-642")`, added by the test-writer, also stays.

## Why there

This fix is test-only by design, and no production source changed. The stale part
was the marker, not the app. ISS-642's analysis rejected the two alternatives:

- `strict=False` keeps a false "unfixed" reason and stops signalling.
- Reverting the parent-uploads seed in
  `agents/capabilities/context_providers/previous_run.py` (`_UPLOADS_MANIFEST` at `:396`)
  deletes a shipped feature.

The blast radius was re-checked by grep before the edit. `ISS-633 unfixed` occurred
once in executable code: this line. The other four `issue("ISS-633")` markers carry
no xfail:

- `tests/integration/agents/test_revision_intelligence.py:404,1152`
- `tests/integration/agents/test_run_revision_fe_contract.py:183,277`

No CI list, config or conftest names the node id.
