# ISS-015

## Title

No generic pipeline-type picker in the UI — **was BY DESIGN; now SHIPPED in Phase 20.** The product launch surface is the hardcoded 6-tile `CreationHub.tsx:15-22` (a closed launchable set); `od_ppt`/`od_prototype` go through the PPT/Prototype wizards (which MUST collect template + design-system → a bare picker structurally can't); bare `prototype`/`ppt` are template-gated-unreachable (D-13-06-a, `websocket.py:1344-1361`); `sample_*` aren't in `SUPPORTED_PIPELINE_TYPES` (test fixtures). The custom-workflow need is already met by the live agent-composer (ISS-014). A user-facing named-manifest picker = WF-DB-01 (v2). **Guardrail (REJECTED hack):** if ever built, a manifest-driven picker MUST read `GET /api/workflows` (manifest discovery — exists, no FE consumer today) + a `user_launchable` manifest flag — NOT a hardcoded dropdown of names (that hack ships broken template-gated buttons + exposes test fixtures). **UPDATE 2026-06-14 — SHIPPED in Phase 20 exactly per this guardrail:** a data-driven catalog reads `GET /api/workflows` + the new additive `user_launchable` manifest flag (zero engine/kernel edits, no new tables), filters by `user_launchable` ∧ tier, reuses CreationHub/AgentModelPicker, and routes prototype/ppt to their wizards — NOT a hardcoded dropdown. INV-3 goldens byte-identical, SC-001 (`WORKFLOWS` const deleted), lint-imports 4/0, verify 5/5.
