# ISS-115

## Title

**Two non-equivalent `## Task N:` parsers still ship (INV-12 debt).** FIX-237 extracted `parseTasks` (`artifactPreview.tsx:80-104`) and pointed the settled tasks CARD at it, but `useWorkflow.ts:1020` keeps its own copy — `/^##\s+Task\s+(\d+)\s*:\s*(.+)$/gm` — which is **line-anchored and tolerates whitespace before the colon**, while the extracted one is neither. They are NOT interchangeable, so unifying them would silently change `protoPlannedTasks`, which feeds `ConstructionBlock` via `AgentThinkingTab.tsx:195-202`. Unifying needs its own change with its own before/after on the construction card — which is exactly why it was filed instead of absorbed.
