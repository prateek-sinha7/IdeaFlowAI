## Symptom

Latent, not reported — found while reasoning about where an override may safely add a step.

The engine sets `ectx.last_streamed = results[-1]["output"]`. Deliverable strategies `ppt` and
`streamed_text` resolve to that, so **the last step's output IS the deliverable**. The canvas
offered a trailing "+" with no warning, so appending an agent to `ppt`, `user_stories` or
`reverse_engineer` replaced the deck/backlog with the new agent's chatter — silently.

Deliverable families:

| strategy | workflows | appending |
|---|---|---|
| `ppt`, `streamed_text` | ppt, user_stories, reverse_engineer | **replaces the deliverable** |
| `single_file`, `serialized_sandbox` | prototype, app_builder, mulesoft, dotnet | safe (sandbox readback) |

## Fix

`CanvasView` disables the append-at-end slot (`insertBeforeId === undefined`) when the **declared**
strategy is `ppt` or `streamed_text`, with a reason naming the file.

**Keyed on the declared strategy deliberately.** The first attempt used
`deliverableStrategy` — which falls back to `?? "streamed_text"` — and so disabled the composer's
own append slot, where appending is the entire point of the surface. `CanvasView.test.tsx` caught
it; the guard now reads `runConfig?.deliverable?.strategy` with no fallback.
