# ISS-962

**INFERRED, not reproduced.** Derived while analysing QA-KB-050 ([BUG-025](20260827-BUG-025.md)). Every link below was
read in source; what has not happened is anyone driving the screen and reading the result. An unreproduced sibling is a

Line numbers are the working tree on 2026-10-02 (HEAD `024c03ef8`). `ComposerPage.tsx`, `AgentsPopup.tsx`,
`DashboardLayout.tsx`, `page.tsx` and `SavedWorkflowsPage.tsx` carry uncommitted edits from other work, so their numbers
can be a few higher than at HEAD; `CanvasNode.tsx`, `AgentRow.tsx`, `CanvasView.tsx` and `AgentLibrary.tsx` are clean
against HEAD. The shared mechanism (the composer reads DashboardLayout's `workflowType`, which is `user_stories` on every
composer route except a built-in's canvas) is proved in [BUG-025](20260827-BUG-025.md)'s analysis section and in
`evidence/qa-kb-050/`; it is not repeated here.

## Condition

The Add-agent panel opened in the composer on `/workflows/new` (and on `/workflows/{id}/edit`), while the composer holds the
shared `workflowType` instead of `custom`.

## Why the root cause implies it (each step read)

1. `ComposerPage.tsx:1706` passes `currentPipelineType={workflowType}` into `AgentLibrary`.
2. `frontend/src/components/workflow/AgentLibrary.tsx:92-97` seeds the panel's tab once, at mount, from that prop:
   `useState(currentPipelineType && KNOWN_CATEGORY_IDS.has(currentPipelineType) ? currentPipelineType : "all")`. `user_stories`
   is a known category, so the panel opens on **User Stories**; with `custom` it would open on **Custom**.
3. `AgentLibrary.tsx:122` is the filter that keeps core agents out of a custom composition:
   `const notHidden = currentPipelineType !== "custom" || !HIDDEN_FROM_CUSTOM.has(agent.id);`. `HIDDEN_FROM_CUSTOM` (`:63-66`) is
   ppt-assembler, backlog-compiler, prototype-finalizer, app-sdlc-governance, ppt-content-strategist, domain-analyst,
   requirements-analyst, material-analyzer. With any non-custom type the filter is a no-op.
4. All eight are in `LOCKED_AGENT_IDS` (`AgentsPopup.tsx:169-181`). The filter exists so a custom composition never holds a
   locked, pipeline-bookend agent. With the stale type they are offered, and the ones that belong to that type's own pipeline
   (domain-analyst and backlog-compiler for `user_stories`) are then locked by `getRole` (Core badge, disabled Remove): the
   "one Core badge" in BUG-025.
5. Consistent with the observation in `tests/integration/DEFECTS-OBSERVED.md:709-739` (D-30): the three agents the e2e added
   from the panel were Estimation Agent, Backlog Architecture Agent and one with a Core badge. In
   `backend/agents/prompts/<id>/AGENT.md` those are story-estimator (`order: 3`), epic-architect (`order: 2`) and domain-analyst
   (`order: 1`, "Domain Discovery Agent", the Core one): the first three agents of the User Stories tab by `order`, which is what a
   panel opened on that tab hands out when "the first agent offered" is added three times (`test_composer_canvas.py:214-224`). What
   is unobserved is the panel's default tab on a cold load; that is the INFERRED part.

## Expected vs actual

- **Expected:** on `/workflows/new` the panel opens on Custom and never lists the eight core agents.
- **Actual (derived):** it opens on the User Stories tab and lists domain-analyst and backlog-compiler (and, on the other
  tabs, the rest of the eight).

## Why this is INFERRED, and what settles it

Steps 1-4 are read. What is unobserved is the rendered panel on a cold `/workflows/new`. Settle it by clicking "Add agent" on
a blank `/workflows/new` and reading the active tab and whether "Domain Discovery Agent" is listed.

## Where the fix belongs

`ComposerPage.tsx:1706` takes the derived `canvasPipelineType` ([BUG-025](20260827-BUG-025.md) fix). Nothing changes inside
`AgentLibrary`: its `custom` branch is the right logic, and `AgentsPopup.tsx:3081` passes its own real type. On a built-in's
canvas `canvasPipelineType` is that built-in's type, so the tab and the (non-)filtering stay as today there.

Offline: mount `ComposerPage` as `renderComposer` does (`ComposerPage.test.tsx:152-181`, default `workflowType` `"user_stories"`)
