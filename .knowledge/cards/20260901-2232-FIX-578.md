## Related

## What was wrong

`deep_agent_runner.py` keeps at most `keep` image blocks in the conversation
and replaces evicted ones with a placeholder. The placeholder told the model to
"re-read it if you need to look at it again". A reader that wanted three pages
with `keep=2` evicted page 1 on reading page 3, read page 1 again on the
placeholder's advice, evicted page 2, and so on — 122 cycles of the same three
PNGs until the provider rejected the request.

## The fix (commit 97717eb9f)

Both placeholders now state the image was already seen and must not be fetched
again, and repeated reads of the same path collapse into a single retained
block, so the cycle cannot start.
