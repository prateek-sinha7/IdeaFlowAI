## Related

## What was wrong

`resolve_launch_od_context` derived a base profile by string manipulation, so
`prototype_feature_revision` resolved to `base_profile =
"prototype_feature_revision"` instead of `"prototype"`. A WEB pipeline was
handed the DECK loader.

## The fix (commit 3e6fcb93c)

The two families are named explicitly; anything else derives its family from
the picked template's own `mode`. Covered by
`backend/tests/unit/test_od_loader_profile.py`.
