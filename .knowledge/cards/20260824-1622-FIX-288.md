Found one layer at a time, each only visible by clicking:

**(a) Blank canvas.** The handoff passed only `agentIds`. `ComposerPage` PREFERS
`initialManifestSteps` and otherwise resolves ids against `ALL_LIBRARY_AGENTS`, where
`custom-agent:emoji` does not exist — every id dropped.

**(b) No button on prototype/ppt.** `AgentsPopup` has TWO mount sites — `IdeaInputPage`
and `LaunchWizard` — and only the first was wired. Wizard-routed types never showed the
control at all.

**(c) Blank again.** `page.tsx` briefly renders `null` through a cold-mount gate after the
`router.push`, which UNMOUNTS `DashboardLayout`. `ComposerPage` mounted twice; the second,
blank mount was the one that survived. The `key={savedComposition?.id ?? "new"}` remount
that rescues saved workflows never fires here, because a built-in has no id.

**(d) "0 agents" after Back.** The sessionStorage workaround for (c) was re-applied to the
LAUNCH panel on the way back, whose `initialAgentIds` restore dropped every composed id —
and the 30s TTL made it survive a refresh.

## Fix

A new route `/workflows/{type}/canvas` carrying the workflow's identity, seeded by a
cold-load fetch of `manifest_steps`. Both entry points navigate there, and the
sessionStorage handoff was DELETED — the URL makes it unnecessary, and it was the cause of
(d).

Decision and the rejected namespace alternative: [ADR-0014](20260824-1630-ADR-0014.md).
