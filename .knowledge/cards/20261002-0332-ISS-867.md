## What is broken

Every test that builds a deck through `app/services/pptx_export.py` gets back
`PPTX generation failed`, with node's own start-up abort:

```
node::InitializeOncePerProcessInternal(...) at src\node.cc:1224
Assertion failed: ncrypto::...
```

The 11 tests in `tests/agents/test_pptx_tools.py` and 2 in
`tests/agents/test_verify_layout.py` (`TestGate`) fail for this reason only.

## Disposition

Open, environment-only. The same 13 ids fail at `75a3c207` and after
[FIX-646](20261002-0332-FIX-646.md). Linux CI is the real check for the pptx gate until
the local Node install is repaired or replaced.
