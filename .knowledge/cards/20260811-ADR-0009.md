# ADR-0009

## Decision

In the context of wanting independent steps to run concurrently, facing a `build_waves`
that partitions Tasks but not Steps and a plan format where most workflows declare no
edges at all, we decided the wave surface is COMPILER-DERIVED (`Step.conflict_keys` from
`instance_id`, `Step.id` aliasing `agent_id`) and that waving is eligible only for plans
carrying declared `depends_on`, to achieve a scheduler that cannot silently parallelize
a workflow whose real ordering it cannot see, accepting that the dispatcher itself and
the per-step context scoping it needs are NOT yet built.

**Area:** engine, workflow, compiler
