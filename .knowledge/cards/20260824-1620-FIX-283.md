`gates: [human]` and `AGENT.md gate: Human_Gate` did opposite things, and which one ran
was decided by whether the step's agent had an `AGENT.md` — invisible from the manifest.

## Implementation

- `HumanGate` registered under BOTH names; the class is identical, only the phase differs.
- `_POST_STEP_GATES` gained `human`; `_HITL_GATES` and `_FAIL_CLOSED_GATES` gained
  `before-human`.
- The inline-gate dedupe was guarded by `name == "human" and phase == "pre"`, which would
  never match once `human` became post-step. It is now phase-agnostic across both HITL
  names — **this is the load-bearing part**: without it `prototype`'s three previously-dead
  `gates: [human]` declarations go live and fire ALONGSIDE the inline gate, double-prompting
  on every prototype run.
- The compiler rejects more than one human-family gate per step.
- `ex_A4_human_gate` and `ex_A4_human_divert` renamed their declarations to `before-human`,
  preserving their behaviour exactly.

## Verified

All 25 manifests compile. `ex_A4_human_gate` still pauses BEFORE `pick-language` showing
the previous step's output; answering it routed to the chosen branch on all three paths.
