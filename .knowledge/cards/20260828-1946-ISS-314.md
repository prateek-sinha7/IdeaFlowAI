## What happened

On `/runs/{id}/preview/full` for a completed run, at a 375px-wide viewport (a
standard mobile width), the Preview tab's secondary toolbar row — holding
"Download"/"Download PPTX" and "Full Screen" — does not wrap, shrink, or
become scrollable. Its container div is:

```tsx
// frontend/src/components/preview/PreviewPanel.tsx:1450
<div className="flex items-center gap-1.5 flex-shrink-0">
```

`flex-shrink-0` on the row prevents it from shrinking to fit, nothing in the
ancestor chain sets `overflow-x: auto/scroll`, and the row's total rendered
content width (~493px) exceeds the 375px viewport. The "Full Screen" button
(rendered at `frontend/src/components/preview/PreviewPanel.tsx:1474-1481`,
`title="Open in new tab"`) is entirely outside the viewport's horizontal
bounds — permanently invisible and unclickable, with no way to scroll it into
view.

## Root cause

`frontend/src/components/preview/PreviewPanel.tsx:1450` — the toolbar row
wrapper (`flex items-center gap-1.5 flex-shrink-0`) has no responsive
behavior at narrow widths: no `flex-wrap`, no shrink-to-fit on its children,
and no `overflow-x` on itself or any ancestor. At viewport widths below the
row's natural content width (~493px), any button after the point of overflow
becomes unreachable rather than wrapping or scrolling into view.

## Expected vs actual

- **Expected:** at narrow/mobile viewport widths, all toolbar controls
  (including "Full Screen") either wrap onto a new line, shrink to fit, or
  live behind a horizontally scrollable/overflow-safe container, so every
  control remains visible and clickable.
- **Actual:** "Full Screen" is pushed completely outside the viewport with no
  scroll mechanism, permanently unreachable at ~375px width. "Download" is
  also partially clipped (right edge 386.6 vs 375px viewport) though still
  mostly clickable.

## Reproduction (3/3, cycle 1)

Reproduced on every one of 3 independent cold-start attempts (fresh
navigation to the URL, viewport re-verified via `window.innerWidth` each
time, per the `viewportUnreliable` quirk in `bug-hunter/velocity.json` —
screenshot pixel dimensions are not trusted):

1. Sign in as `qa-admin@flowinqa.com`.
2. Navigate directly to
   `http://localhost:3000/runs/b9feac1c-ec21-4531-8ba7-bb391786993e/preview/full`
   (a completed run; Preview tab auto-selected).
3. Set viewport to 375x700, confirmed via `window.innerWidth === 375`.
4. Query `Full Screen` button's `getBoundingClientRect()`.

All 3 attempts (cold navigation each time, one via a fully blanked page
first) produced the identical rect: `left: 400.02, right: 493.38, top:
455.20, bottom: 482.80`, against `window.innerWidth: 375`. Confirmed via
`document.elementFromPoint(innerWidth-1, 460)` resolving to the "Download"
button, not "Full Screen" — no pointer/touch interaction at the viewport's
right edge can reach it. `document.documentElement.scrollWidth === clientWidth
=== 375` on every attempt, confirming no horizontal scroll exists anywhere
in the ancestor chain.

No axis variance was needed — this reproduced identically on cycle 1, 2 and
3 with no narrowing required.
