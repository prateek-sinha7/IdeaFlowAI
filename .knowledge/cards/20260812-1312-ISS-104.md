# ISS-104

## Title

**Stale line-number citations in the shutdown-path comments — each points at code that has since moved, and a fixer following them lands in the wrong place.** `backend/docker-entrypoint.sh:27` cites `docker-compose.yml:138` for `stop_grace_period`, which is at `docker-compose.yml:75`. `backend/app/api/run_shutdown.py:30` cites `engine.py:5263` for the `NON_TERMINAL` set, which is at `engine.py:5838-5841` (the same wrong cite appears on the ISS-089 row as `engine.py:5710-5713`). `run_shutdown.py:97-98` cites `run_commands.py:1401` for the Concierge hold and `:1356` for the durable `chat_reply` persist. `run_shutdown.py:275` cites `run_commands.py:2155` / `:2473` for the driver terminal writes; the `CancelledError` handlers are at `:2424-2435` and `:2654-2659`. Filed rather than folded into FIX-234: they are in files that change touched, but they are a separate class of defect (documentation drift) and fixing them inside a behaviour change would blur the diff. The equivalent cite inside the block FIX-234 was mandated to rewrite (`config.py:197` → `docker-compose.yml:75`) WAS corrected there.
