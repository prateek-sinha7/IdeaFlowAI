# ISS-1186

Sibling of [ISS-1158](20261009-1510-ISS-1158.md) (register entry QA-KB-122). Derived from its root cause, not yet run: S-11-17 and S-11-18 are two of the 12 tests that fail on the `disposable_user` 422, and the register treats them as fixed by the same one-key change. They are not. Filed 2026-10-09 by the analyzer.

## Condition

Both tests were written for the pre-cut-over reset model and assert three things the backend no longer has:

| what the test sends / expects | where | what the code does now |
|---|---|---|
| body `{"new_password": "flowin-e2e-temporary-1"}` (S-11-17), `{"new_password": ..., "permanent": True}` (S-11-18) | `suites/11_admin/test_admin.py:456`, `:495` | `ResetPasswordRequest` has no fields and `extra="forbid"` (`backend/app/api/admin.py:197-205`): 422 for both. The frontend sends `{}` (`frontend/src/lib/api.ts:2113-2123`). |
| a 200 body carrying `requires_new_password_at_next_login` | `test_admin.py:461`, `:500` | the endpoint answers `{"status": "temporary_password_set"}` (`admin.py:555`) |
| a refusal containing "local break-glass account and cannot be reset here" | `BREAK_GLASS_REFUSAL`, `test_admin.py:424`, asserted at `:467`, `:506` | the string occurs nowhere under `backend/app`; `b2a621f84` deleted the break-glass account, and migration 0038 dropped `auth_provider`, which their docstrings describe ("A user created here comes back `auth_provider: \"local\"`") |

## Why the root cause implies it

Same Phase 3, Decision 6 model as the create endpoint: "This endpoint issues a TEMPORARY password ... No password is supplied by the admin" (`admin.py:197-203`). The unit tier met the same change in QA-KB-077: [ISS-904](20261001-2149-ISS-904.md) found `test_iss401_reset_password_message.py` testing the deleted break-glass reset, and [FIX-736](20261007-1940-FIX-736.md) deleted it. The live copies stayed.

## Expected vs actual

Expected after the fixture body is fixed: S-11-17 and S-11-18 still fail, at the reset call (422 `extra_forbidden`, loc `body.new_password`), not at the fixture. Actual today: they fail earlier, at the fixture's 422, which hides this (the four fixture 422s of `11_admin` are at `rerun/11_admin.log:82,231,280,323`).

## Fix direction (test-side only)

- S-11-17: rewrite to the real contract. `POST /api/admin/users/{id}/reset-password` with `{}` on a disposable user answers 200 `{"status": "temporary_password_set"}` and leaks no credential. It acts on a throwaway account in the live pool, which is what the endpoint does.
- Pin the property the decision exists for: a body that carries `new_password` or `permanent` answers 422 `extra_forbidden`. That is the useful remainder of S-11-18; the "permanent reset" the test and `tests/integration/screens/11-admin.feature.md:64-84` and `:236-260` describe no longer exists, so delete that scenario text rather than keep a test for it.
- Do not widen `ResetPasswordRequest` or have the endpoint return the temporary password to make the old assertions pass.

## Confidence

CONFIRMED by reading both sides: the request model, the response literal, the frontend call, the three test sites, and the absence of the refusal text. INFERRED, not run: that both tests stay red after the fixture is fixed and that the rewritten S-11-17 passes (the reset hits live Cognito `AdminSetUserPassword` on a throwaway user).
