## FIX-229 — the one real defect

`pipeline_cancelled`?"* and read the answer as *"a later attempt succeeded"*. Two different
questions: on a gate rejection both events belong to **one** attempt. Probe-captured tail:


Now requires a `run_resuming`/`pipeline_start` attempt boundary between the two.

## Filed, not fixed — ISS-091

**Rejecting at a gate does not STOP the pipeline.** Measured at `99fcf4a2`, i.e. **with
FIX-227 present** — so FIX-227 does not cover it. Probe on a normal launch with a rejecting
inline `Human_Gate`:

```
AFTER pipeline_cancelled: [wave_started, subagent_spawned ×2, subagent_result ×2,
 merge_started, merge_completed, wave_completed,  wave_started, subagent_spawned ×2,
 subagent_result ×2, merge_started, merge_completed, wave_completed, pipeline_complete]
```

`_run_agent`'s `return` ends only its own generator; the consuming loop
(`engine.py:2513-2520`) observes `agent_error` and nothing else; and the step-boundary check
that would stop it (`:2371`) reads `cancel_event`, which a gate rejection never sets — which
is exactly why FIX-227's mechanism is orthogonal. Steps routed through `_run_agent` are
spared by its terminal-state guard (`:3319`); **`wave_scheduler` is not, and has zero
`cancel_event`/terminal checks** — that is also the true remainder of FIX-227's
"four deferred loops" note (`engine.py:2371` and `task_loop.py:275/:376` **do** check).
