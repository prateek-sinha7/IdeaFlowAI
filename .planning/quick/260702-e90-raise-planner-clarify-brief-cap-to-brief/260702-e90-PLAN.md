<objective>
Raise the planner + clarify brief-INPUT caps to a single tunable `settings.BRIEF_MAX_CHARS`
(64k) so realistic briefs — including a full uploaded document — reach the SmartPlanner
(`_build_prompt` coverage scan + `missing_information`) and the ClarifyEngine (`brief_sample`)
IN FULL, instead of being silently whittled to head[:1500]+tail[-500:] (planner analyze) /
`brief[:3000]` (stored user_request) / `user_request[:2000]` (clarify).

Both planner and clarify read the SAME setting — one source of truth, aligned with the 64k
upload cap in `file_extract._MAX_TEXT_CHARS`. A full 64k brief is ~16k input tokens for a
SINGLE planner/clarify LLM call — trivial within Haiku's 200k context; no token-loop concern.

Purpose: stop the AI from analyzing/clarifying a truncated brief; the middle of a long brief
(and any full uploaded doc) currently never reaches the model.
Output: `BRIEF_MAX_CHARS` in config; three cap sites routed through it; a new regression test;
INV-3 goldens proven byte/event-identical with no snapshot regeneration.
</objective>
