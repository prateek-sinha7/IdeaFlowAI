# Phase quick-260702-07w Plan 01: Keep PlanningOverlay visible across planner-clarify gap Summary

Keyed the DashboardLayout right-panel render-gate ternary off `(pipelineState?.agents?.length ?? 0) === 0` instead of `pipelineState?.plannerStatus === "running"`, so the existing `PlanningOverlay` stays up through the slow clarify question-generation gap instead of vanishing at `planner_complete`.

## What Was Built

A single-term condition change in the PlanningOverlay render gate (`frontend/src/components/layout/DashboardLayout.tsx`, ~line 1442):

- **Before:** `pipelineState?.plannerStatus === "running"` — flips false at `planner_complete`, leaving a blank PreviewPanel during the clarify LLM gap.
- **After:** `(pipelineState?.agents?.length ?? 0) === 0` — stays true from launch until `pipeline_start` seeds domain agents, spanning the entire `planner_start -> questionnaire_ready` window.

`pipelineState.agents` is `[]` at launch (`useWorkflow.startPipeline`) and is only populated at the `pipeline_start` event, with nothing in between mutating it. The overlay therefore drops exactly at:
- `pipeline_start` (agents seeded, proceed-without-clarify path), or
- `questionnaire_ready` (the existing `questionnaireQuestions.length === 0` guard drops the overlay and QuestionnairePanel takes over).

The adjacent comment on line 1440 was updated to "while planner or clarify is working (no domain agents yet)". No new component, state, copy, backend, or test/golden changes.
