## Accomplishments
- **PART A:** New `_BedrockCachePointsMiddleware` (mirrors `_ToolFilterMiddleware`: sync `wrap_model_call` + async `awrap_model_call` + `request.override`), Bedrock-only via `isinstance(request.model, ChatBedrockConverse)`, gated on `settings.BEDROCK_PROMPT_CACHE_ENABLED` (read at call time), sets `model_settings['cache_control']={"type":"ephemeral","ttl":settings.BEDROCK_PROMPT_CACHE_TTL}`.
