<objective>
Cluster D (WR-04, WR-06) + Cluster E CR-06 — drive revision behavior off a DECLARED signal
and evict the kernel-resident revision block. Today `_is_revision_workflow` (engine.py:646)
keys on `"previous_run" in compiled.context_providers` — a provider-name proxy that
misclassifies the four other workflows declaring `previous_run` as prototype revisions
(masked only by the secondary `prototype.html.is_file()` guard), and the seed + `assert_owns`
(engine.py:1071, previous_run.py:44-55) gate only on `parent_run_id` presence. And the whole
prototype-revision flow — HTML extraction, `prototype.html` seeding, pre-edit baseline,
post-edit fix-loop, hardwired `agent_id="prototype-revision-agent"`, the const
`REVISION_FILE_NAME` — lives inside the kernel `execute()` with a self-described "DELIBERATE
EXCEPTION." This plan: (1) add a declared revision-intent flag to the manifest/compiled model;
(2) source `is_revision_workflow` from THAT flag, not provider presence; (3) re-couple the
previous_run seed + `assert_owns` to the declared signal, parameterized by `deliverable.name`;
(4) move seed-existing-artifact into the `previous_run` provider and the pre-edit baseline +
post-edit fix-loop into a declared post-step capability; (5) remove the DELIBERATE EXCEPTION.

Purpose: a provider-name proxy is a workflow-identity branch in disguise (INV-1 altitude
violation) and the kernel-resident block is the exact name-by-name behavior SC-001 forbids.
Driving revision off a declared flag closes the misclassification (WR-04/WR-06) and moving the
block to capabilities removes the last hand-wired prototype-revision leak (CR-06).

Output: a declared revision-intent flag; a kernel that no longer hosts the revision block; a
previous_run provider + post-step capability that own the revision behavior; prototype_revision
at parity; L16 ownership still enforced.
</objective>
