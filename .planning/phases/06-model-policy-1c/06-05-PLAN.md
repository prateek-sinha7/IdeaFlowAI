<objective>
Implement the model-switch fallback layer (APPROACH B — engine-level rebuild-and-retry, NOT `with_fallbacks`) above the existing botocore retries (MODEL-02). On a classified transient throttle the runner re-raises (B1), the engine advances `ctx.model_resolver` to the next chain id, rebuilds via `create_runner`, and re-invokes — bounded by chain length. Chain exhaustion re-raises the last error. Unit-tested with a scripted throttle (no live Bedrock).

Purpose: This is the one genuinely-new mechanism of the phase. RESEARCH resolved the D-05 binary to APPROACH B: `with_fallbacks` does NOT compose through the deepagents `astream_events` graph (RunnableWithFallbacks lacks bind_tools; the runner swallows model exceptions). The engine owns the retry loop; `build_model` stays the unchanged single source.
Output: `deep_agent_runner.py` (B1 re-raise of classified throttles), `engine.py` (rebuild-and-retry loop), `tests/agents/_scripted_model.py` (throttle variant), `tests/agents/test_model_fallback.py` (new). Characterization unchanged (no-throttle path identical).
</objective>
