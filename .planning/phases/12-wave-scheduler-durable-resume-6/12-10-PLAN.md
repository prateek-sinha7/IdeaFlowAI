workflow ACTUALLY produces. The repo already provides the `serialized_sandbox`

the run sandbox into the `filename:`-block bundle — i.e. exactly the merged
part_a/b/c/d.txt base the wave workers + copy_disjoint produce. Switching sample_wave's
deliverable to `serialized_sandbox` makes the declared deliverable resolve from real
produced files (no fallback warning), is purely a manifest change, and keeps the
already-registered-capabilities-only constraint (serialized_sandbox is registered).
