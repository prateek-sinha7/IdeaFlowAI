(screenshot 51-runE2-stuck.png: "0/2 agents" + Stop while backend status=completed).
Fix: add a `pipeline_reconnected` handler that, on live:false + terminal status,
resolves the run; on live:false + non-terminal status, schedules a bounded re-reconnect.

of WAVE-03's FE half) and stop the post-resume "running forever" hang (RESUME-04 FE
recovery). The 12-09 backend bridge plan makes pipeline_reconnected.status non-null
and adds an engine→WS live-attach path; this plan makes the FE consume both the
non-live terminal status and (once 12-09 lands) the live re-attach.
