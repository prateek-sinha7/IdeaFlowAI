<objective>
D-15/C — the phase's ONE architectural change: promote template/design-system from a prototype-hardcoded run-launch branch to a DECLARED run input any deliverable can opt into. Additive and BOUNDARY-ONLY.

Today the launch boundary decides whether to load template/DS od_context by branching on the workflow-name literals `pipeline_type == "od_prototype"` / `== "od_ppt"`, duplicated in two functions: `run_commands.py::_resolve_launch_agents` (REST) and `websocket.py::_handle_workflow_execution` (WS). That is the SC-001/INV-1 leak. Replace the name-branch with a check on the DECLARED manifest signal `context_providers: [opendesign]` (Option A per RESEARCH — reuses an existing declaration, zero new manifest key). Because prototype/od_ppt already declare `opendesign`, their od_context build is unchanged by construction, so the 5 goldens stay byte-identical (INV-3).

Purpose: close the milestone core-value gap (a new deliverable gains template/DS acceptance by declaring one manifest line, with zero engine/boundary edit) while proving prototype/PPT launch behavior is byte- and event-identical.
Output: a shared app-side seam `resolve_launch_od_context()`, both boundaries rewired to it, and a NEW launch-boundary od_context-parity test.
</objective>
