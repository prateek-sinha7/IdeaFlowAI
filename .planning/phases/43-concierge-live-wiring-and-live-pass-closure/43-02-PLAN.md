Wire the dormant Concierge to the settled-run chat lane (the CRUX, A.1 FE half). Today
`handleFreeText` (RunChatLane.tsx:646-655) treats EVERY settled-run free-text turn as a
revision trigger — "what's the status?" launches a `prototype_revision`. Attach the
already-declared-but-unwired Concierge props at the mount (DashboardLayout.tsx:1660), extend
the `useRunChat.sendMessage` contract to carry the `concierge` / `confirm_proposal` fields on
the `POST /messages` payload, and re-route the free-text path so an ASK is answered by the
Concierge while a CHANGE REQUEST still routes to `onRevise`.

Purpose: A status question must be answered, not launched as a run. Classification is GENERIC
(INV-1) — no workflow-name/agent-id branch. Per the A.0 RESOLVED decision the SSE transport
stays OFF this plan (LOCK-B): the FE targets the `POST /messages` contract but the actual
transport flip is the supervised Part-C cutover (43-06). So this plan proves the routing
DECISION offline (unit tests), not a live round-trip.

Output: Concierge props wired, the send contract extended, a generic ask-vs-change classifier,
and confirm-chip POSTs — all `tsc --noEmit` clean and covered by targeted vitest.
