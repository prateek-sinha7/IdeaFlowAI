<objective>
Build the thin no-DSL `WorkflowCompiler` (`compile(manifest, registry) -> CompiledWorkflow`: name-resolve every capability reference against the registry, topo-validate the Step DAG, nothing more — MAN-02/MAN-03/INV-1/INV-4/INV-5) and hand-author all 15 `workflow.yaml` manifests (one per `PIPELINE_AGENTS` key, D-03/D-05) grounded in `PIPELINE_AGENTS` + the per-manifest reference map. Then lock the two parity traps with dedicated tests: `clarify.defaults` reproduce `engine._pipeline_defaults` verbatim, and every dispatchable manifest declares `planner: run` (because `SKIP_PLANNER_FOR_PROTOTYPE = False` today). All 15 load+compile (zero exemptions); `reverse_engineer` -> empty plan; `chat` -> compiles but engine-non-dispatchable.

Purpose: this plan delivers the compiler + the data it consumes, and proves byte-identity-preserving parity before the engine routes off it (04-04). The manifests faithfully reproducing the hardcoded dicts is the #1 parity risk (RESEARCH Key Insight).

Output: `agents/workflows/compiler.py`, 15 `agents/workflows/<id>/workflow.yaml`, and `tests/agents/{test_compiler,test_manifest_coverage,test_manifest_parity,test_edge_manifests}.py`.
</objective>
