<objective>
Reclaim `/api/workflows` for manifest-derived metadata (API-01 / D-01) and relocate the existing run-history CRUD to a new `/api/runs` router (D-02), atomically repointing the 10 frontend refs (clean break, no aliases — RESEARCH D-02 confirmed no external API-key consumer). The rewritten `GET /api/workflows` lists every authored workflow (id, name, description, step summary) and `GET /api/workflows/{id}` returns the full compiled step configs (gates, validators, deliverable, declared capabilities) with 404 on an unknown id, all derived from the compiled manifests. The relocated `/api/runs/*` routes preserve the per-user `user_id == current_user.id` ownership filter on every query and carry over the export-pptx filename sanitization verbatim.

Purpose: the composer reads workflows dynamically from the manifests instead of hardcoded types (API-01), and the run-history sub-resource home (`/api/runs`) is established early as the §22 plan envisions (Phase 5 lands `/api/runs/{id}/artifacts|events` there).

Output: `app/api/runs.py` (new), rewritten `app/api/workflows.py`, `app/main.py` mount, 4 frontend files repointed, and `tests/unit/{test_workflows_api,test_runs_api}.py`.
</objective>
