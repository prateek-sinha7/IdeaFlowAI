<objective>
Route the engine to execute from the `CompiledWorkflow` (MAN-04) and reduce `pipeline_type` to an id-alias (MAN-05). At run entry, resolve `pipeline_type` to a manifest id (`od_prototype` -> `prototype`; identity otherwise), load+compile that manifest, and source the FOUR routing concerns from the compiled plan: step sequence + agent IDs (replacing `get_pipeline_agents` as the source), deliverable spec, clarify defaults (replacing `_pipeline_defaults`), and planner-skip (replacing `SKIP_PLANNER_FOR_PROTOTYPE and is_prototype_pipeline`). The surviving L1-L13 behavioral branches (the 8 `pipeline_type` + 3 `spec.id` branches per the D-09 classification) stay BYTE-IDENTICAL and are allow-listed as Phase-7-scoped. No legacy dispatch fallback. `accumulated_outputs` mirror unchanged.

This is the MVP vertical slice for this infra phase: after this plan, `prototype` (and the other 12 dispatchable pipelines + the `od_prototype` alias) runs end-to-end from `manifest -> compiler -> CompiledWorkflow -> registry-validated -> engine routed`, proven green against the Phase-0A characterization snapshot (SC-001 / INV-3).

Purpose: this is the seam that makes the declaration layer load-bearing. Until the engine reads the compiled plan, the manifests are inert data.

Output: the `engine.py` routing seam + id-alias resolver, and `tests/agents/{test_id_alias_resolver,test_pipeline_type_routing,test_compiled_plan_runs}.py`.
</objective>
