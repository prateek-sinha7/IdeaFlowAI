READING IT
----------
Line 4 is the tell: the SECOND `handleSelectWorkflowRun` is called from
`DashboardPage.useEffect` — the deep-link reopen effect at page.tsx:3453-3552.
It is supposed to skip a click-driven open (`if (runId === trackedRunIdRef.current)
return;`, :3462, "Skipping here is what keeps a client-side nav from /runs from
double-fetching (AC3)") but `trackedRunIdRef` is a useRef in the page component
and the click's own router.push remounts that component — page.tsx:3480-3484
says so in as many words — so the ref is null again by the time the effect runs.

Consequence: the entire reopen (getWorkflow + events?after=0 + the chat-seed
getRunFamily) runs twice, and DashboardLayout's contentSourceRunId effect
(ISS-804's second /family call site) runs once per reopen. 2 mechanisms x 2
reopens = the 4 /family calls above, and ISS-785's measured "/events x2".
