How to write one short, punchy sentence about the word:

- Keep it under 12 words. Cut every word that isn't pulling weight.
- Casual voice — contractions ("it's", "that's"), no formal connectors ("furthermore",
  "thus").
- One vivid, concrete image or comparison, not an abstract description.
- End on the word itself or right next to it, never buried in the middle.
- No rhyme, no poem structure — this is prose, just lively prose.
