How to build a couplet that actually rhymes:

- Two lines. The LAST word of line 1 and line 2 must rhyme on their final vowel sound.
- Keep both lines a similar length — that is what makes it scan.
- Prefer concrete images ("a desk, a mug, a name") over abstractions ("synergy, journey").
