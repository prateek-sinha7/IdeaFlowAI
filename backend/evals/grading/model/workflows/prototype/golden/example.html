Grounding · Signal mapping & ideation

Grounded in the live work — not assumption

The reimagined process is derived from connected observation of the actual work across the connected systems. Every step, status, and the matching signature itself is learned from what the connectors see the work do.

Observed, then connected

Connectors mirror the real steps the team performs — the process spine is measured, not imagined.

Signature is learned

The matching signature — the pattern of an actionable item — is derived from historical outcomes, not a hand-written rule.

A diff, not a rebuild

The future state is expressed as a clean diff over the observed baseline — one new step, two updates, nothing removed.
