✨ aha moment

💡 The truth is

It's not that you slept too little,
it's that you woke up at the wrong time.

Your body runs on 90-minute cycles.
Get yanked awake mid "deep sleep" by an alarm
and even 9 hours of sleep can feel like none at all.

The key is: wake up right at a cycle boundary.

5 / 9
1

Work backward from wake-up time

👉 The formula

Wake-up time − 90min × N − 15min to fall asleep
= the time you should get in bed tonight

Example: waking up at 7am

→ bed at 11:15pm (4 cycles)
→ bed at 12:45am (3 cycles)

2

10 minutes of morning light

☀️ Open the curtains / take a short walk

The moment natural light hits you, melatonin production stops and you actually wake up. Works on cloudy days too — no skipping it.

⏰ stronger than coffee

Lazy person's version:

Stand by the window while you brush your teeth 🪥

A 20-minute nap
at 3pm

⏱️ 20 minutes max. Go past 30 and you'll drop into deep sleep, waking up even more tired.

💡 Pro tip: Drink a sip of coffee right before you nap. The caffeine kicks in right around 20 minutes later, stacking with your nap's alertness boost.
