Four little escapes
03 · 08

Four small things
you can hand entirely to AI

01

Replying to "got it" emails

They don't need your brain. Let AI handle them in your tone — save 40 minutes a week.

02

Booking restaurants, rebooking flights, checking routes

Outsource it with one sentence. You just pick the final option, not dig through ten apps.

03

Turning meeting recordings into action items

Recording → summary → to-dos, done in one click. You just confirm and sign off.

04

Sorting the 300 photos you took last week

Group by event, pick the best 10, write captions. Archive-tidying is finally automated.

My auto-reply prompt
05 · 08

The prompt
that automates "got it" emails

# auto-reply skill
when email matches "got it / sounds good / confirmed / thanks, received":
  reply:
    tone: "gentle, brief, not too corporate"
    max_lines: 2
    sign_with: "— Lewis"

always_skip:
  - from: ["family", "partner", "close friends"]
  - contains: ["urgent", "contract", "payment"]

# saves 38 minutes a week, tested
